Kinetix gives every service its own language on purpose: one domain, twelve idioms, and contracts
that have to hold between all of them. There are two exceptions, each for its domain. notary shares Go with
search because its domain needs go-ethereum, and its contracts on chain are Solidity. seal shares TypeScript
with identity because Midnight's only application SDK is TypeScript, and its contracts are Compact. The versions below are the ones pinned in each repository's
manifest or lockfile, not the ones its README mentions; a version with no evidence in the repository
is left out rather than guessed.

## Services

Each service owns its database and is reached only through gRPC from other services, or through the
gateway from outside.

## Edge, contracts and clients

The gateway is the only door from the internet. The contracts are the only definition of a message
on the wire. The courier app and the backoffice are the clients Kinetix builds itself: one for couriers on
their phones, one for staff in a browser.
