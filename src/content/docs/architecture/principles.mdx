A principle that nothing enforces is a wish. Each rule below links to where the code holds it in place,
at a fixed commit, so you can check that it is still true.

## 1. Every piece of data has one owner

Each service owns its tables and its database, and no other service reads them. A service that needs
another's data asks the owner over gRPC and gets the owner's answer, not a copy that may be stale or
forged.

catalog shows live stock by
[asking warehouse](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/776bc6acb74c48b45fa9b30a98a2a12609b104ab/core/infrastructure/grpc/bin_stock_client.py#L44),
and order prices a checkout by
[asking catalog](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/82834065c34981f1c69a88b3d3317d20396358d9/Infrastructure/Grpc/CatalogProductDirectory.cs#L22)
for each product. The [system map](/architecture/system-map/) draws every one of these questions.

## 2. A message is defined once

Every message that crosses a service boundary is defined in `kinetix-contracts`, in Protocol Buffers,
and every service generates its code from there instead of keeping its own copy. A change that would
break a running caller — a field number reused, a type narrowed —
[fails the contracts build](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-contracts/blob/cbd5d56df761b920ce1125d755d02c7c1f2f0491/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L49-L88)
unless it is written down as an intended break.

## 3. There is one door from outside

Browsers, apps and integrations reach Kinetix only through the
[Kong gateway](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-api-gateway/blob/bb836a1020e40cf739ad7b579d499b79f6245906/config/gateway.pkl),
which decides which paths are public, which need a token, and how fast a caller may go. A service with
no route there — notification, communication — cannot be reached from the internet at all.

## 4. Only identity decides who is calling

Accounts and passwords live in identity and nowhere else, and identity is the only service that issues
access tokens. It signs them with RS256 and publishes its public keys; every other service
[accepts only RS256 and checks the signature against those keys](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/377490ff62a3a0d3393c15fb7f55995fef21a2a9/lib/kinetix/token_verifier.rb#L35-L66)
itself, without calling identity on every request. A service that wants to know more about a caller —
a merchant's status, a principal's name — asks identity over gRPC.

## 5. Services prove who they are to each other

Calls between services travel over mutual TLS, and each service's certificate carries a SPIFFE
identity. Every gRPC server in the estate keeps a list of the services allowed to call it —
[required, with no default](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/82834065c34981f1c69a88b3d3317d20396358d9/Security/PeerAuthorizationInterceptor.cs#L8-L27)
— and
[refuses any caller not on it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/82834065c34981f1c69a88b3d3317d20396358d9/Security/PeerAuthorizationInterceptor.cs#L96-L105),
whatever its network address. Being inside the network is not a credential.

## 6. When in doubt, refuse

A decision that depends on an answer the system could not get is not made with a guess. If pricing is
unreachable, a checkout is
[refused with `PRICING_UNAVAILABLE`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/82834065c34981f1c69a88b3d3317d20396358d9/Controllers/OrderController.cs#L38-L43)
rather than charged at a remembered or default price; a missing setting stops a service at start-up
rather than letting it run on a fallback.

## 7. Anything that moves money or stock can be repeated safely

Networks retry, users double-click, sweepers run again. So every operation that takes money or stock
carries a key that makes a repeat harmless: checkout honours an
[`Idempotency-Key`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/82834065c34981f1c69a88b3d3317d20396358d9/Controllers/OrderController.cs#L31),
warehouse
[records every reservation against its key](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/377490ff62a3a0d3393c15fb7f55995fef21a2a9/app/services/inventory/idempotent_operation.rb#L77-L116)
and replays the first answer, and pricing keeps a ledger row per order that a second redeem finds
instead of counting twice. [The checkout saga](/learn/checkout-saga/) depends on all of them.

## 8. A failure is never reported as an answer

"Nothing found", "out of stock" and "could not ask" are different facts, and the system keeps them
apart. A refused checkout answers
[409 with the reason](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/82834065c34981f1c69a88b3d3317d20396358d9/Controllers/OrderController.cs#L90-L95),
an unreachable dependency 503, an internal fault 502; warehouse names
[which row a timed-out wait was on](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/377490ff62a3a0d3393c15fb7f55995fef21a2a9/app/services/inventory/idempotent_operation.rb#L102-L116).
A failure dressed as a definite business answer — "no stock" when the stock service simply did not
answer — sends people to fix the wrong thing, which is why it is treated as a defect in its own right.
