Kinetix holds other people's money: buyers' funds in escrow until the return window closes, merchant
and driver balances, customer wallets. Every one of those numbers lives in the payment service's
database, which nobody outside Kinetix can read. The notary makes the platform's side of them
checkable from the outside, without trusting Kinetix to report on itself.

## What is committed

Every hour the notary takes payment's snapshot of what the platform owes and anchors a commitment to it as
one epoch in a ledger contract on Base Sepolia. The way it commits has changed once. Each epoch keeps the
scheme it was anchored under:

- **Epochs 0 to 182, the first generation (scheme 1).** The notary builds a Merkle sum tree. Each leaf is an
  account's commitment and amount, and each node carries the hash and the sum of the two below it, so the
  root fixes every balance and the total at once. The ledger stores the root, the total and the number of
  accounts
  ([`anchor`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/LiabilityLedger.sol#L50-L89)).
- **From epoch 183, the second generation (scheme 2, since 2026-10-20).** The total is no longer published.
  The leaves are hashed with Poseidon2 into a tree of fixed depth 8, which holds up to 256 accounts. The
  ledger stores the tree's root and `C`, a commitment to the total under a random blinding that the notary
  keeps. A zero-knowledge proof (PLONK) shows three things without revealing any amount: every amount is a
  64-bit unsigned integer, the leaves hash to that root, and their sum is the total that `C` commits to
  ([the circuit](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/plonk/circuit.go#L34-L91)).
  The ledger runs the proof's verifier itself
  ([`proveEpoch`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/confidential/LiabilityLedgerV2.sol#L75-L115)),
  and it anchors an epoch only after that epoch's proof has passed
  ([`anchor`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/confidential/LiabilityLedgerV2.sol#L117-L166)).

The ledger takes each epoch once. A second epoch with the same number is refused. Every epoch's digest
includes the digest before it, so an anchored epoch cannot be changed, dropped or reordered. The second
generation starts where the first was sealed: its first epoch follows the first generation's last digest.
The ledger directory names both generations, so a proof of either is verified through the same address
([`LedgerDirectory`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/directory/LedgerDirectory.sol#L39-L58)).

An account appears in the tree only as a commitment: a hash of the account kind, a salt and the holder, and,
in later epochs, of a secret that only the account's holder is given. The first generation's tree shows the
amounts and the total, but not whose they are. The second generation publishes neither: on chain an epoch is
the tree's root, `C` and the number of accounts.

Anchoring takes two keys: the notary's, and an independent watch that checks the snapshot against
payment's own record of it before it approves. The ledger accepts an epoch only from the council that
holds both
([`AnchorCouncil.anchor`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/council/AnchorCouncil.sol#L53-L59),
and in the second generation
[`ConfidentialAnchorCouncil.anchor`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/confidential/ConfidentialAnchorCouncil.sol#L54-L60)).
For a second-generation epoch the watch rebuilds the tree's root and `C` itself. It signs only after the
ledger reports that the epoch's proof has passed
([the watch's check](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/approving/approver.go#L260-L286)).

## What a proof shows

A proof opens one account's commitment and gives the sibling hashes on the path from its leaf to the root.
In the first generation each sibling also carries its sum. In the second, every proof carries exactly eight
siblings and no sums, because the sums are proven inside the zero-knowledge proof and never published.
When a proof verifies against an anchored, final epoch, it shows that:

- Kinetix committed, publicly and at a recorded time, to one specific set of balances and their exact
  total. In the second generation the total stays behind `C`, and the zero-knowledge proof shows that
  it is the sum of those balances;
- this account's balance is in that set, at that amount;
- the commitment cannot be changed afterwards.

The verifier computes the path itself and asks the ledger contract to compute it too
([`verifyInclusion`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/LiabilityLedger.sol#L102-L120),
and in the second generation
[`verifyInclusion`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/confidential/LiabilityLedgerV2.sol#L181-L204)).
It gives a verdict only when both agree.

## What it does not show

- **Solvency.** The money behind these liabilities is held by a payment provider and a bank, off chain.
  Nothing on chain says it can all be paid out. A proof of reserves would need those custodians to
  attest to it, and they do not.
- **That every account is included.** An account nobody checks could be left out without anyone
  noticing. The defence is that anyone can check any epoch at any time, and that a lie or a silence
  about it can be proved, as below.
- **That payment's numbers are right.** The tree commits to whatever the snapshot says. If a balance is
  wrong in payment, the notary anchors the wrong balance faithfully.
- **The second generation's total.** The notary keeps each epoch's blinding, so it can open `C` to an
  auditor, and nobody else can read it
  ([decision 47](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L1715)).
- **That your amount stays between you and Kinetix.** In the first generation the epoch total is public.
  In both generations, checking a proof through an RPC endpoint tells that endpoint the commitment and
  amount being checked.

## When the notary lies or stays silent

A proof also carries a statement: its commitment, amount and path, signed by the key the statement
court has registered as the signer for that epoch. Each generation has its own statement court and
withholding court, and the directory names them beside the ledger. That turns two failures into evidence
that anyone can put on chain:

- **A false proof.** If a signed proof does not verify, the court verifies it again on chain and records
  a fault against the epoch ([`proveBadProof`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/court/StatementCourt.sol#L59-L78)).
  Two different signed statements for one epoch that name the same commitment, or the same position in
  the tree, contradict each other and are recorded the same way ([`proveEquivocation`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/court/StatementCourt.sol#L80-L101)).
- **Silence.** A holder who registered an address can ask the withholding court for their statement for
  an epoch, within a week of its anchoring
  ([`requestStatement`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/withholding/WithholdingCourt.sol#L49-L91)). If the notary has
  not answered within 24 hours, anyone may record the silence
  ([`recordUnanswered`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/withholding/WithholdingCourt.sol#L121-L146)).
  A registration is made for one withholding court, so a holder registers again for the second generation.

A recorded fault or silence is never cleared. The verifier reports how many faults an epoch carries
beside every verdict. Putting a fault or a request on chain costs gas, which the paymaster of the epoch's
generation pays, so a holder needs a key and no ETH.

To check a proof of your own, see [Verify a proof of liabilities](/verify/proof-of-liabilities/).
