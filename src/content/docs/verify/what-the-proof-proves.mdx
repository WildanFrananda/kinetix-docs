Kinetix holds other people's money: buyers' funds in escrow until the return window closes, merchant
and driver balances, customer wallets. Every one of those numbers lives in the payment service's
database, which nobody outside Kinetix can read. The notary makes the platform's side of them
checkable from the outside, without trusting Kinetix to report on itself.

## What is committed

Every hour the notary takes payment's snapshot of what the platform owes and builds a Merkle sum tree
over it. Each leaf is an account's commitment and amount; each node carries the hash and the sum of the
two below it, so the root fixes every balance and the total at once. The notary anchors the root, the
total and the number of accounts as one epoch in the ledger contract on Base Sepolia.

The ledger takes each epoch once. A second epoch with the same number is refused, and every epoch's
digest includes the digest before it, so an anchored epoch cannot be changed, dropped or reordered
([`anchor`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/7d292fa763b17f3ad5742734aeeced126e670b6a/contracts/src/LiabilityLedger.sol#L50-L89)).

An account appears in the tree only as a commitment: a hash of the account kind, a salt and the holder,
and in later epochs of a secret that only the account's holder is given. The tree shows the amounts and
the total, not whose they are.

Anchoring takes two keys: the notary's, and an independent watch that checks the snapshot against
payment's own record of it before it approves. The ledger accepts an epoch only from the council that
holds both ([`AnchorCouncil.anchor`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/7d292fa763b17f3ad5742734aeeced126e670b6a/contracts/src/council/AnchorCouncil.sol#L53-L59)).

## What a proof shows

A proof opens one account's commitment and gives the sibling hashes and sums on the path from its leaf
to the root. When it verifies against an anchored, final epoch, it shows that:

- Kinetix committed, publicly and at a recorded time, to one specific set of balances and their exact
  total;
- this account's balance is in that set, at that amount;
- the commitment cannot be changed afterwards.

The verifier computes the path itself and asks the ledger contract to compute it too
([`verifyInclusion`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/7d292fa763b17f3ad5742734aeeced126e670b6a/contracts/src/LiabilityLedger.sol#L102-L120)). It gives a verdict only when
both agree.

## What it does not show

- **Solvency.** The money behind these liabilities is held by a payment provider and a bank, off chain.
  Nothing on chain says it can all be paid out. A proof of reserves would need those custodians to
  attest to it, and they do not.
- **That every account is included.** An account nobody checks could be left out without anyone
  noticing. The defence is that anyone can check any epoch at any time, and that a lie or a silence
  about it can be proved, as below.
- **That payment's numbers are right.** The tree commits to whatever the snapshot says. If a balance is
  wrong in payment, the notary anchors the wrong balance faithfully.
- **That your amount stays between you and Kinetix.** The epoch total is public, and checking a proof
  through an RPC endpoint tells that endpoint the commitment and amount being checked.

## When the notary lies or stays silent

A proof also carries a statement: its commitment, amount and path, signed by the key the statement
court has registered as the signer for that epoch. That turns two failures into evidence that anyone can
put on chain:

- **A false proof.** If a signed proof does not verify, the court verifies it again on chain and records
  a fault against the epoch ([`proveBadProof`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/7d292fa763b17f3ad5742734aeeced126e670b6a/contracts/src/court/StatementCourt.sol#L59-L78)).
  Two different signed statements for one epoch that name the same commitment, or the same position in
  the tree, contradict each other and are recorded the same way ([`proveEquivocation`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/7d292fa763b17f3ad5742734aeeced126e670b6a/contracts/src/court/StatementCourt.sol#L80-L101)).
- **Silence.** A holder who registered an address can ask the withholding court for their statement for
  an epoch, within a week of its anchoring
  ([`requestStatement`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/7d292fa763b17f3ad5742734aeeced126e670b6a/contracts/src/withholding/WithholdingCourt.sol#L49-L91)). If the notary has
  not answered within 24 hours, anyone may record the silence
  ([`recordUnanswered`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/7d292fa763b17f3ad5742734aeeced126e670b6a/contracts/src/withholding/WithholdingCourt.sol#L121-L146)).

A recorded fault or silence is never cleared. The verifier reports how many faults an epoch carries
beside every verdict. Putting a fault or a request on chain costs gas, which the notary's paymaster
pays, so a holder needs a key and no ETH.

To check a proof of your own, see [Verify a proof of liabilities](/verify/proof-of-liabilities/).
