Order, matching and review each keep an append-only log of what happened: every status an order reaches,
every delivery with the SHA-256 of its photo, every review as it was written. Once a day the notary commits
to each log's new records and anchors the commitment on Base Sepolia. This page checks that a record of an
order or a review you are part of is inside one of those commitments, unchanged. The verifier runs in your
browser and reads the chain through an RPC endpoint you choose, so no Kinetix server is asked anything.

What the evidence shows, and what it cannot show, is explained in
[What the evidence proves](/verify/what-the-evidence-proves/).

## Get your proofs

Ask the notary for the proofs of an order or of a review, with your access token:

```http
GET /api/v1/proofs/evidence/orders/{order}
GET /api/v1/proofs/evidence/reviews/{review}
Authorization: Bearer <access token>
```

`{order}` is the order number and `{review}` the review's id
([the routes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/httpapi/evidence_proofs.go#L14-L19)).
You get the records you are a party to
([the rules](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/evidencing/prover.go#L19-L79)):

| Record | Who gets its proof |
| --- | --- |
| a status change of an order, or a step of its return | the buyer, the merchant, and whoever made the change ([parties](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/notary/lifecycle_record.go#L26-L28)) |
| the delivery of an order | the courier who delivered it, and the order's buyer and merchant |
| a product review | its author and the merchant |
| a rating of a courier | its author and the courier rated: review logs no merchant for a rating ([parties](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/notary/review_record.go#L26-L32), [review's log](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/d259ba53f8ce03651eec94f8fe4e1faf1544c6b2/database/migrations/2026_10_16_000001_reviews_keep_an_evidence_log.php#L101-L112)) |

Anyone else gets `404` with `no_evidence`, the same answer as for an order the notary has no record of
([the codes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/httpapi/evidence_proofs.go#L106-L121)).

The answer is `{"proofs": [...], "pending": n}`. Each entry of `proofs` is one record with its salt and its
path to the root of an anchored, final batch. `pending` counts the records of yours that are not in a final
batch yet ([the count](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/evidencing/prover.go#L81-L134)):
a stream's batch closes a day after the one before it, and the chain takes about twenty minutes more to
finalize it, so a record's proof can take up to a day to appear. Save the whole answer as `evidence.json`. The verifier takes it as
it is, or any one document from its `proofs` list.

A proof carries the record itself: the order number and the people in it, the statuses, the times, the
photo's SHA-256, a review's rating and the SHA-256 of its comment. Keep the file to yourself as you would the
order.

## Verify it here

<NotaryVerifier page="evidence" />

This is the same verifier as on [Verify a proof of liabilities](/verify/proof-of-liabilities/):
`notary-verify` compiled to WebAssembly, release <code>{notaryVerifier.release}</code>, checked against its
SHA-256 before it runs. It tells the two kinds of document apart by their `format` and hands an evidence
document to the evidence verifier
([the entry point](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/cmd/notary-verify-wasm/main.go#L86-L132)).

The record's fields are hashed in the browser and never sent. The endpoint is asked about the batch, and given
the record's digest, its salt and its path, because the registry contract checks the proof a second time on
its own ([`verifyInclusion`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/evidence/EvidenceRegistry.sol#L156-L175)).
The salt is what keeps a guess at a record from being confirmed against the chain, so if the endpoint should
not hold it, point the verifier at a node of your own.

## Read the answer

The page prints one verdict for each proof and the exit code `notary-verify` prints for the same document
([the verification](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/verifying/evidence_verifier.go#L18-L123),
[the codes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/verifycli/exit.go#L3-L18)).
With several proofs, the exit code is that of the first one that is not `included`
([`RunEvidence`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/verifycli/evidence.go#L37-L59)).

| Verdict | Exit | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `included` | 0 | the batch is final on chain, and the record, with its salt, leads to the batch's root |
| `rejected` | 1 | the batch is on chain, but the record does not lead to its root; `reason` says which check failed |
| `not_anchored` | 2 | the registry holds no such batch for the record's stream |
| `not_final` | 3 | the batch is anchored, but the chain has not finalized it yet ([how it is told](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/ethereum/evidence_registry.go#L158-L208)) |
| `unknown` | 4 | the chain could not be asked; nothing is known, and it is not a rejection |
| `wrong_ledger` | 5 | the proof was issued against another registry, or another chain, than the one given |
| `disagreement` | 6 | the verifier and the registry contract computed different answers, so neither is given |
| `malformed_proof` | 65 | the text is not an evidence document |

Beside the verdict come the record's `stream` (`order_lifecycle`, `delivery` or `review`), its `batch`, when
the batch closed and when it was anchored, and the record's place in its owner's log as a transaction and a
sequence. Only `included` means the record is in the commitment.

## Check a photo, a signature or a comment

The proof holds hashes, not files. A delivery's `photoSha256` and `signatureSha256` are the SHA-256 of the
images the courier uploaded, and a review's `commentSha256` is the SHA-256 of its comment's UTF-8 bytes. To
show that a photo you hold is the one the delivery was recorded with, hash it and compare:

```sh
sha256sum photo.jpg
printf '%s' 'the comment, exactly as written' | sha256sum
```

The output is the hash without its `0x`. When it matches and the proof is `included`, those exact bytes
existed when the batch closed and have not changed since.

## Verify it without this page

The same verifier is published as a container image for amd64 and arm64
([the command](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/cmd/notary-verify/main.go#L40-L42)):

```sh
docker run --rm -i \
  -e KINETIX_NOTARY_RPC_URL=https://sepolia.base.org \
  -e KINETIX_NOTARY_CHAIN_ID=84532 \
  -e KINETIX_NOTARY_EVIDENCE_REGISTRY_ADDRESS=0xbF1F15F40cA8Dd63c070d223243febf6731EF480 \
  -e KINETIX_NOTARY_RPC_TIMEOUT=20s \
  ghcr.io/wildanfrananda/kinetix-notary-cli:v1.6.1 \
  notary-verify /dev/stdin < evidence.json
```

It prints one JSON line for each proof and exits with the code from the table above. To check that this page
runs what the repository says, build the WebAssembly yourself as
[Verify a proof of liabilities](/verify/proof-of-liabilities/#verify-it-without-this-page) shows.
