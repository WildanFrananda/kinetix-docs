identity owns every account on Kinetix — customers, sellers, couriers and admins — and everything that
says who someone is: passwords and second factors, the tokens every other service trusts, the principal
id each person is known by across the estate, the merchant a seller trades as and whether it may sell,
and the profile and address a courier is sent to. No other service stores a password or signs a token;
when one needs to know who it is talking to, it verifies identity's signature or asks identity.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against identity [v1.1.0](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/tree/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb),
the version in production.

## What it serves over gRPC

Other services ask identity about people and merchants: whether a merchant may sell and where its store
is, where a customer lives, who a principal is, and which merchants have changed since a cursor. That
cursor is the database transaction that last changed a merchant, recorded by a trigger in a table of its own,
and the feed
[serves only merchants written by transactions older than every transaction still running](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/infrastructure/persistence/adapters/typeorm_merchant_repository.adapter.ts#L94-L122),
so a merchant whose transaction commits late is served late and never skipped. A cursor without a
transaction is still read the old way, by the time a merchant last changed.

## What it calls

identity calls out for two things: to file a new courier's vehicle in matching's fleet and to activate
the driver when an admin approves them, and to place an address on the map.

## One principal per person

Every account is given a principal — a UUID that is the same person in every service. Other services store
the principal, never identity's own account number, so a service can be rebuilt or a database restored
without people swapping places. identity keeps the link from each service-local id to its principal as an
alias:
[the first time an account is issued a token, at registration, its principal is minted](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/principal_resolver.service.ts#L20-L51),
and when a seller is approved their merchant row is aliased to the same principal. An alias
[cannot be re-pointed](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/principal_resolver.service.ts#L63-L82)
at another principal once it exists. Access tokens carry the principal as their subject.

## Signing in and tokens

- **Passwords** are bcrypt hashes. A sign-in for an email nobody registered
  [still runs a comparison](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/auth_usecase.service.ts#L30-L52),
  so the answer takes as long as for a wrong password and does not reveal which accounts exist. Sign-in,
  registration and the second-factor check are limited to five attempts a minute.
- **A second factor**, once enabled, turns the password into half a sign-in: the password earns a
  five-minute challenge, and only
  [the challenge with a valid code](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/auth_usecase.service.ts#L54-L70)
  earns tokens.
- **Tokens** are RS256 JWTs, and the public keys are published at `/.well-known/jwks.json` for every
  other service to verify against: the signing key and, while a rotation is under way,
  [the keys that signed before it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/infrastructure/crypto/jwt_key_provider.ts#L114-L137), which verify
  but never sign. Identity checks a token with
  [the key its `kid` names](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/token.service.ts#L259-L282) and refuses one that names
  no key it holds, so a new signing key does not sign everyone out. An access token lives fifteen minutes; a refresh token lives thirty
  days, is stored, and
  [works once](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/token.service.ts#L167-L213)
  — using it again revokes every token in its session. Signing out
  [revokes the access token and the session](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/token.service.ts#L215-L222).
  The whole design, and the two flaws found while documenting it, is in
  [Tokens that can be taken back](/learn/tokens-that-can-be-taken-back/).
- **The role in a token** is read from the account each time a token is issued or refreshed, so a role an
  admin grants takes effect at the next refresh.

## Roles, and how an account gets one

Registration always creates a customer —
[the role is not part of the request](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/auth_usecase.service.ts#L72-L85).
The other roles are granted:

- **A seller** files an application with a store name, business registration, tax id and store address;
  it stays `pending` until
  [an admin approves it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/seller_onboarding_usecase.service.ts#L104-L161),
  which creates the merchant, places the store address on the map, and makes the account a seller. A
  merchant
  [may sell only while it is `verified` or `active`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/domain/merchant_standing.ts#L1-L5),
  and every service that lets a merchant sell — checkout, promotions, products, fulfilment — asks.
- **A courier** registers with a vehicle, which identity files in matching's fleet; the account stays a
  customer until
  [an admin approves it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/courier_onboarding_usecase.service.ts#L28-L70),
  which makes it a courier and activates the driver.
- **One account holds one role.** Approval never overwrites a role: a courier or an admin
  [is refused a seller application](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/application/services/seller_onboarding_usecase.service.ts#L163-L171),
  and only a customer can be approved as a courier. A person who is both uses two accounts.
- **Admins** are not created through the API. The first one is made by a one-off script that refuses to
  run once any admin exists.

## Unknown is not "not found"

`GetMerchantInfo`, `GetUserProfile` and `ValidateToken` answer "no such merchant", "no such person" or
"invalid token" only when that is true. When identity cannot read its own database the call fails with
[`UNAVAILABLE`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/adapters/controllers/identity_grpc.controller.ts#L390-L400),
and every caller treats that as "identity could not say": order refuses a checkout with `503` rather than
telling the buyer the merchant may not sell, and notification retries a message rather than dropping it.
The lookups used to answer "not found" for any failure, which turned a slow database into a definite
business answer — see [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/). A person who has an account and
never filled in a profile is found, with empty fields; that is an answer, not a failure.

## How the code is laid out

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/adapters/` | the HTTP and gRPC controllers, the guards that decide who may call a route, and the mappers onto the contract |
| `src/application/` | the use cases — sign-in and tokens, seller and courier onboarding, profiles, geocoding, principals |
| `src/domain/` | entities, the ports the use cases need, and the rule for which merchant may sell — no NestJS, no TypeORM |
| `src/infrastructure/` | what implements the ports: TypeORM persistence, the signing key, TOTP, the Nominatim geocoder, the fleet client, the mesh identity, observability |
| `src/migrations/` | TypeORM migrations, run by `migrate.ts` |

Most use cases work through ports and are tested with fakes; the token service and the principal
resolver use TypeORM repositories directly, and their races are tested on a real PostgreSQL database.

## Data

PostgreSQL, through TypeORM, holds accounts and profiles, principals and their aliases, merchants and
seller applications, the transaction that last changed each merchant, the refresh tokens of every session
and the access tokens revoked before they expire, and every address already placed on the map, so a second lookup of the same address does not ask
the geocoder again. The schema is changed only by migrations, which
[the same image runs as `migrate.js`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/migrate.ts#L3-L23)
in a one-shot container that must finish before the service starts.

## Who may do what

- **Without a token**: signing in, registering as a customer or a courier, refreshing, completing a
  second factor, the key set, health and metrics. Everything else
  [needs an access token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/app.module.ts#L115-L118)
  — a guard on every route, which a route has to opt out of rather than in.
- **An account's own resources** — its profile, its seller application — are
  [reachable by that account or an admin](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/adapters/guards/self_or_staff.guard.ts#L41-L49),
  and nobody else.
- **A second factor is the owner's alone.**
  [Setting one up, enabling and disabling it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/adapters/controllers/auth.controller.ts#L71-L101)
  are refused even to an admin: setting one up hands back its secret, and a second factor someone else
  holds is not a second factor.
- **Approving a seller or a courier** takes the `admin` role.
- **Other services** reach the gRPC port only with a certificate from Kinetix's CA —
  [the handshake requires one](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/infrastructure/mesh/service_identity.ts#L25-L31)
  — naming a service on identity's allow list, and
  [each is allowed only the calls it makes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/infrastructure/mesh/peer_allow_list.ts#L48-L63):
  order resolves principals, reads merchants and profiles, and places the address a buyer typed at
  checkout; warehouse, catalog and pricing read a merchant; notification reads a profile; search follows the
  merchant change feed. A service that may ask whether a merchant may sell cannot read a customer's address,
  no running service may validate a token or read a principal over gRPC, and nobody may geocode addresses in
  bulk — communication, in the table above, is not deployed, and is granted nothing until it is. Any other call
  [is refused with `PERMISSION_DENIED` and logged](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/infrastructure/mesh/peer_authorization_interceptor.ts#L27-L35);
  see [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

- `/health` answers when the process is up; `/health/ready`
  [runs a real query](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/adapters/controllers/health.controller.ts#L27-L40),
  so the container is healthy only when the service can serve.
- `/metrics` serves Prometheus text with the names the platform contract gives identity.
- The database reads behind the gRPC lookups
  [have a five-second deadline](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/infrastructure/persistence/with_timeout.ts#L1-L10),
  so a slow database becomes `UNAVAILABLE` instead of a call that never returns.
- On `SIGTERM` both servers stop taking work and finish what they hold, within
  [a 25-second budget](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/40b7e4f76a3f151100f1d48dd9253126ee16ddcb/src/infrastructure/observability/shutdown_drain.ts#L5).
