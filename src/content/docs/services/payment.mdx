payment owns the wallets — a customer's balance, a merchant's and a courier's earnings — the top-ups
that put money into a customer's wallet through Midtrans, and the escrow that holds a checkout's money
between the buyer and the people it is owed to. It decides none of the timing. Order tells it when to
hold, when to pay the courier, when to refund and when to pay the merchant; payment's job is to move
exactly that money, once, and to refuse anything that would not add up.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against payment [v0.1.10](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/tree/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695),
the version in production.

## What it serves over gRPC

Escrow is reached only over gRPC, and only order calls it: to hold the money at checkout, and later to
settle, refund or release it as the order's life plays out.

## What it calls

payment calls out only to Midtrans: to create the charge for a top-up, and to ask whether it was paid.

## How a checkout's money moves

1. **Held at checkout.** `CreateEscrowHold`
   [debits the customer's wallet the order's total](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L328-L385)
   — or refuses with `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` — and adds the merchant's share to the merchant's pending
   balance. The shipping fee is pending for the courier; at checkout no courier has been dispatched yet,
   so it waits in a holding account for unassigned couriers' fees. A hold
   [must balance](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/domain/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/domain/entity/EscrowHold.java#L46-L78):
   the total is the merchant's share plus the fee, or nothing is held.
2. **The courier is paid on delivery.** When matching reports a delivery, order calls
   `SettleShippingFee` naming the courier who made it, and
   [the fee moves to that courier's wallet](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L149-L224)
   from wherever it is held. A settled fee cannot be pointed at a second courier, and a refunded order
   has no fee left to pay. If the merchant was paid before the courier's report arrived, the fee waited
   in the holding account, and
   [only a fee that order put there](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L226-L237)
   is paid out of it — the account holds every order's fees together, and paying one that was never kept
   there would spend another courier's.
3. **Returned goods are refunded from the merchant's share.** `RefundGoods`
   [lowers the merchant's pending balance and credits the customer](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L290-L322),
   only while the money is still held, and
   [never for more than the merchant still has coming](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/domain/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/domain/entity/EscrowHold.java#L88-L118).
   The shipping fee is never part of it. How much a return is worth is order's calculation — see
   [order's returns](/services/order/#returns).
4. **The merchant is paid when the order completes.** `ReleaseEscrow`
   [moves what is left of the merchant's share to their available balance](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L446-L498),
   and a fee no courier has claimed yet becomes available in the holding account. payment has no timer
   of its own: order asks for the release once the return window has closed — see
   [When the merchant is paid](/services/order/#when-the-merchant-is-paid).
5. **A cancelled order is refunded whole.** `RefundEscrow`
   [gives the customer back what is still held](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L557-L558)
   — the total less any goods already refunded — and cancels the merchant's and the courier's pending
   amounts. It is refused once the merchant has been paid, and
   [once the courier has been paid](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L542-L548)
   for a delivery that happened: refunding the fee then would take it from no account at all.

A wallet never goes below zero to make a movement fit. Moving money out of a balance that does not hold
it
[throws](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/domain/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/domain/entity/EscrowAmount.java#L15-L23)
instead of clamping, because a clamp turns a bookkeeping error into money that quietly stops existing.
Every movement also writes a ledger row under
[a reference that names it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L631-L650)
— `escrow:hold:{order}`, `escrow:release:{order}`, `escrow:goods:{order}:{refunded so far}` and so on —
and the reference is unique, so one movement cannot be recorded twice.

## Moving money exactly once

Every escrow call can be retried, so every one is idempotent:

- **A key and a fingerprint.** order sends an idempotency key with each call; one that arrives without a
  key [is keyed by its order number](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L706-L716).
  payment records a fingerprint of the request with the key, and a repeat with the same fingerprint
  answers `already_applied` without moving anything. The same key with a different request
  [is refused](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L652-L665)
  as `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` — see [The same request, twice](/learn/idempotency/).
- **Settlement needs no key.** The hold remembers which courier it paid, so a repeat for the same courier
  is a no-op and one for another courier is refused.
- **A refund that overtakes its hold is not lost.** If `RefundEscrow` arrives before the hold it undoes,
  it is remembered: a hold that commits afterwards is unwound by the refund's retry, and a hold that
  arrives later still
  [is refused](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L336-L340).
  This is the case the [checkout saga](/learn/checkout-saga/) creates when a compensation races the step
  it compensates.
- **One order at a time.** Everything for one order runs in a transaction that first takes
  [a PostgreSQL advisory lock on the order number](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L75-L80),
  so two calls for the same order never interleave. The wallets it touches are then locked by owner
  [in a fixed order](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/EscrowService.java#L698-L704),
  so two orders that share a merchant cannot deadlock each other.

## Top-ups

A customer pays for checkouts from their wallet, and fills it through Midtrans:

1. **The request** names an amount — [whole rupiah, at least IDR 1,000](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/TopUpService.java#L280-L314)
   — and a method: a bank virtual account, which needs a bank, or QRIS, which takes none. An
   `Idempotency-Key` header is required.
2. **The top-up is recorded as `PENDING` before Midtrans is called**, under the customer and the key, so
   [a retry returns the same top-up](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/TopUpService.java#L65-L91)
   and its payment instructions instead of charging twice. If Midtrans gives no definite answer, the
   top-up stays pending: the charge may exist, so the client is told to ask for its status rather than
   start another.
3. **Midtrans's notification is a prompt, not a fact.** Its signature is
   [checked in constant time](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/infrastructure/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/infrastructure/gateway/MidtransPaymentGatewayAdapter.java#L135-L151),
   and even a valid one only
   [makes payment ask Midtrans's status API](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/TopUpService.java#L134-L181).
   The wallet is credited only when Midtrans says the charge settled
   [for exactly the amount that was charged](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/TopUpService.java#L183-L204);
   any other amount credits nothing and is left for a person. See
   [Never trust a number the client can type](/learn/trust-boundaries/).
4. **A notification that never comes** does not leave the money in limbo. Every five minutes,
   [top-ups pending for longer than ten minutes are asked about](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/api/scheduling/ReconciliationScheduler.java#L47-L63),
   a hundred at a time. If Midtrans cannot be reached, nothing is decided and the top-up is asked about
   again — see [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/).

## How the code is laid out

| Module | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `modules/domain/` | wallets, escrow holds and ledger entries, the ports the application needs, and the domain's exceptions — no Spring, no JPA |
| `modules/application/` | `EscrowService`, `TopUpService` and `WalletService`: the rules on this page, against those ports |
| `modules/infrastructure/` | what implements the ports: JPA persistence, the advisory lock, the Midtrans adapter, and the gRPC server with its caller check |
| `modules/api/` | the Spring Boot application: HTTP controllers, token validation, the reconciliation schedule, health and metrics, the Liquibase changelog |

The escrow rules are tested against fakes of the ports, and again on a real PostgreSQL database, where
unique constraints, row locks and advisory locks behave as they do in production.

## Data

PostgreSQL, through JPA, holds the customer, merchant and courier wallets, the holding account for
unassigned couriers' fees, escrow holds, the idempotency keys and fingerprints of escrow calls, and one ledger of
transactions — top-ups and every escrow movement. The schema is
[a Liquibase changelog](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/resources/db/changelog/db.changelog-master.yaml)
generated from the JPA entities, applied by the same image under
[the `migrate` profile](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/resources/application-migrate.yml)
in a one-shot container that must finish before the service starts. The service itself only validates
the schema it finds.

## Who may do what

- **The wallet routes** take an identity access token —
  [RS256, from identity's key set, for Kinetix's audience, and only an access token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/api/security/SecurityConfig.java#L45-L65);
  see [Tokens that can be taken back](/learn/tokens-that-can-be-taken-back/).
- **Each route reads the caller's own wallet.** None takes an account to look at: the wallet is the one
  belonging to the principal in the token, so no token reads anyone else's balance. The role
  [decides which routes it reaches](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/api/controller/WalletController.java#L75-L83)
  — a customer the customer balance and top-ups, a seller the merchant balance, a courier the courier
  balance. A top-up's status is
  [shown only to the customer who started it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/TopUpService.java#L93-L101).
- **Midtrans's notification** needs no token, because Midtrans sends it. Its signature is what admits
  it, and all it can do is make payment ask Midtrans.
- **No HTTP route moves escrow money.** Holding, settling, refunding and releasing are order's to ask
  for, over gRPC, because each one rests on a fact order owns — that the parcel arrived, that the window
  closed. A person's word over HTTP would decide those facts in the wrong place.
- **Other services** reach the gRPC port only with a certificate from Kinetix's CA —
  [the handshake requires one](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/resources/application.yml#L99-L105)
  — and only if it names
  [a service on payment's allow list](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/infrastructure/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/infrastructure/grpc/PeerAuthorizationInterceptor.java#L70-L100),
  which in production holds order alone; see [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

- `/health` answers when the process is up; `/health/ready`
  [runs a real query](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/api/controller/HealthController.java#L31-L48)
  and turns `503` while the instance drains after `SIGTERM`.
- `/metrics` serves Prometheus text with the names the platform contract gives payment.
- An escrow transaction
  [waits at most three seconds for a lock and runs for at most four](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/infrastructure/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/infrastructure/persistence/SpringTransactionRunner.java#L15-L16).
  A call that loses that race
  [is answered `ABORTED`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/infrastructure/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/infrastructure/grpc/PaymentGrpcServerService.java#L219-L246),
  which means try again. A refusal is `FAILED_PRECONDITION`, `NOT_FOUND` or `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, with the
  reason in an error detail, and trying again will not change it.
- Kinetix uses Midtrans's sandbox, in production as well. Moving to another environment is
  [the base URL and the server key](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/afc4f61b28905705a6227e2c085ed04b2703a695/modules/api/src/main/resources/application.yml#L35-L44)
  and nothing else, and every call to Midtrans is bounded — five seconds to connect, fifteen to answer.
