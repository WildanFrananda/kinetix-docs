pricing owns promotions — discounts, vouchers and flash sales — and the shipping rate card. It owns no
product price and stores no total: order hands it the lines of a cart at catalog's prices and the journey
matching quoted, and pricing answers with what the buyer pays. It also keeps the two ledgers that make a
voucher's quota and a flash sale's stock impossible to spend twice.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against pricing [v0.1.10](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/tree/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80),
the version in production.

## What it serves over gRPC

Only order calls pricing over gRPC: before a checkout, to quote shipping and price the cart; during the
checkout saga, to spend a voucher's quota and a flash sale's stock, and to give them back when a later
step fails.

## What it calls

pricing calls out only when a seller files a promotion — identity, to learn which merchant the seller
trades as and whether it may sell, and catalog, to learn who owns the product a promotion names.

## How a price is calculated

1. **Each line starts at the base price order sends**, which is catalog's price for the product, never
   the price stored in the cart — see [Never trust a number the client can type](/learn/trust-boundaries/).
2. **A flash sale or a discount lowers the unit price.**
   [A running flash sale that is cheaper wins](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/services/pricing_service.rs#L156-L189);
   otherwise the discount that saves the most, among those that name the product, its category, or
   nothing at all.
3. **A voucher is checked against the subtotal** —
   [active, unexpired, with quota left and the minimum spend met](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/traits/voucher_evaluator.rs#L15-L43)
   — and takes a percentage, capped at its maximum, or a fixed amount, never more than the subtotal. A
   promotion of any kind other than `PERCENTAGE` or `FIXED` applies nothing.
4. **Shipping comes from the rate card** for the tier matching quoted:
   [a base fee, plus a fee per kilometre, per kilogram, and per kilogram per hundred kilometres](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/models/shipping_rate.rs#L43-L59),
   rounded to the cent. A tier with no rate
   [is refused](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/services/pricing_service.rs#L107-L129),
   not priced at nothing.
5. **The total** is the subtotal, less the voucher, plus shipping. A voucher discounts the goods only: the
   shipping fee is what the courier is paid, and no promotion reduces it.

A promotion that cannot be read does not quietly drop out of the price. If the database does not answer,
the calculation fails, and order refuses the checkout instead of charging a total that left the voucher
out — see [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/).

## Promotions and who owns them

Every promotion has an owner, and
[the owner decides which carts it reaches](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/traits/promotion_scope.rs#L1-L6):

- **A promotion an admin creates belongs to the platform** and applies to every cart.
- **A promotion a seller creates belongs to their merchant** — the merchant identity names for that
  seller, and only while identity says it may sell — and applies only to that merchant's carts. A cart
  holds one merchant's products, because order refuses a cart that spans two.
- **A flash sale, or a discount that names a product, must name the seller's own product**, as catalog
  records it. A seller's category discount needs no such check: it can only reach the seller's own cart.

[These checks](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/services/promotion_service.rs#L25-L76)
run before anything is written. If identity or catalog cannot be asked, the promotion is refused with
`503` rather than filed on a guess.

## Spending a promotion exactly once

`RedeemVoucher` and `AllocateFlashSaleStock` each run in one transaction and write a ledger row per order,
so a retried call finds its own row and answers as before instead of spending again. The spend itself is
[one guarded statement](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/repositories/quota_repository.rs#L78-L94):

```sql
UPDATE vouchers
SET used_count = used_count + 1
WHERE code = $1
  AND active
  AND used_count < quota
```

The decision is made in the database, against the row as it is when the statement runs. When two
checkouts race for a voucher's last use, the second waits on the first one's row lock and then re-checks
the condition against the committed row — which no longer passes, so it updates nothing and is refused.
This is the same race as in [Two buyers, one item](/learn/two-buyers-one-item/), closed by deciding in
the statement rather than in the application. A voucher owned by one merchant is refused outright for
another merchant's order.

The release calls mark the ledger row released and give the use or the units back, which is how the
checkout saga undoes a step — see [The checkout saga](/learn/checkout-saga/).

## How the code is laid out

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/routes/` | the HTTP surface: creating and reading promotions, health, metrics |
| `src/grpc/` | the gRPC server order calls |
| `src/services/` | the price calculation, who may own a promotion, the voucher preview |
| `src/traits/` | the discount and voucher evaluators, and which carts a promotion reaches |
| `src/repositories/` | Diesel repositories behind ports, and the two ledgers |
| `src/clients/` | the gRPC clients for identity and catalog, behind ports |
| `src/security/` | service identity on the mesh, the caller allow list, token verification |
| `migrations/` | SQL migrations, embedded in the binary |

The price calculation depends on repository ports and the ownership rules on directory ports, not on
Diesel or gRPC, so both are tested with fakes and no database.

## Data

PostgreSQL, through Diesel, holds
[discounts, vouchers and flash sales, each with its owning merchant or none; the voucher redemption and
flash-sale allocation ledgers; and the shipping rate card](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/schema.rs).
The migrations are compiled into the binary and
[run by the same image with `--migrate`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/main.rs#L45-L59),
in a one-shot container that must finish before the service starts.

## Who may do what

- **Creating a promotion** takes an identity access token —
  [only an access token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/security/jwt.rs#L180-L182),
  see [Tokens that can be taken back](/learn/tokens-that-can-be-taken-back/) — with
  [the `admin` or `seller` role](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/guards/auth_guard.rs#L72-L99),
  and then the ownership checks above.
- **Reading** the active discounts, a voucher by its code and a product's flash sale needs no token. So
  does the voucher preview, which takes the cart's merchant with the code and the subtotal and answers as
  checkout would.
- **Other services** reach the gRPC port only with a certificate from Kinetix's CA naming a service on
  pricing's allow list, and pricing checks the name on every server it calls in return; see
  [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

- `/health` answers when the process is up; `/health/ready`
  [runs a real query](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/routes/health_routes.rs#L16-L38),
  so the container is healthy only when the service can serve.
- `/metrics` serves Prometheus text with the names the platform contract gives pricing.
- Every call to identity and catalog
  [has a five-second deadline](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/clients/identity_merchant_directory.rs#L11),
  so a peer that hangs becomes a refusal instead of a stuck request.
- On `SIGTERM` both servers stop taking work and finish what they hold, within
  [a 25-second budget](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/76e134d02c6bffba658eede7db7553d5b108bc80/src/main.rs#L37).
