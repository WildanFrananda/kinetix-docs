A courier who wants to show a new customer, a lender or another platform that they are reliable today has two
choices: hand over their whole record, or ask to be trusted. seal gives them a third. It issues the courier a
credential that states their deliveries, their average rating and how long they have been delivering, records it
in a contract on Midnight, and the courier's own phone proves a claim about it — "at least 500 deliveries, rated
4.5 or better" — without sending the numbers anywhere.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against seal [v1.9.1](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/tree/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf),
the version in production. Every request and answer is in the REST reference: the courier's side in
[Credentials](/apis/rest/courier-app/credentials/), the staff's in [Predicates](/apis/rest/backoffice/predicates/).

## At a glance

<ServiceFacts service="seal" />

seal is written in TypeScript and runs on Node. Its contracts are written in Compact, Midnight's language for
contracts whose inputs stay private: the
[credential registry](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/contracts/src/credential_registry/CredentialRegistry.compact)
is the one that runs. It serves nothing over gRPC; couriers, verifiers and staff reach it through the gateway.

## What it calls

<ServiceCalls service="seal" />

seal reads the same two logs as the [notary](/services/notary/): matching's deliveries and review's reviews.
It
[keeps only the reviews of drivers](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/application/courier_facts.service.ts#L83-L92),
and it checks every page before recording it. A feed that does not answer
[is read again on the next tick](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/application/courier_facts.service.ts#L103-L118);
a malformed page stops that stream and is logged as an error. Access tokens are checked against identity's
published keys, so that fetch is not an arrow on the map.

## A credential is a snapshot

When a courier asks for a credential, seal does not call matching or review. It reads its own copy of their
facts, and refuses unless
[the caller is a courier and both feeds caught up recently](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/application/courier_credentials.service.ts#L87-L94):
a feed that is down or behind would otherwise state an old count as today's. The credential holds
[four numbers](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/domain/courier_credential_attributes.ts#L4-L13):
the deliveries, the average rating in hundredths, the days since the first delivery, and the day it was
issued. A courier with no delivery gets none.

A courier gets
[one credential a day](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/application/courier_credentials.service.ts#L96-L101).
Asking again with the same key returns that day's credential, and asking with another key is refused. The key is
a public key the phone derives from a secret that never leaves it.

## The phone proves, the contract checks

A verifier does not choose what to ask. It picks a predicate that staff approved — minimums for the first three
numbers and how recent the credential must be — and the contract's
[`present` circuit](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/contracts/src/credential_registry/CredentialRegistry.compact#L50-L68)
looks the minimums up by the predicate's id. A verifier free to choose its own thresholds could narrow down a
courier's exact numbers by asking many times; one that can only pick an approved predicate cannot. The circuit
proves that the credential was issued to the holder of the secret, that it is of the predicate's class, and that
each number meets its minimum, then records the verifier's challenge as answered.

The proof is made on the courier's phone. seal serves the page the phone proves with and a snapshot of the
registry to prove against, and never sees the secret or the proof's inputs.

## Who pays, and who relays

Every transaction on Midnight pays a fee in DUST. The courier pays nothing: the verifier hands the proven
transaction to seal's relay, which
[accepts only a presentation](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/domain/presentation_shape.ts#L3-L23)
— exactly one call, to `present`, on the active registry, moving no coins — and
[asks the sponsor to pay for it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/application/presentation_relay.service.ts#L20-L45).
Because the verifier relays, seal sees the verifier's address at presentation time and never the courier's.

The sponsor is seal's second process, started from the same image by
[its own entry point](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/sponsor_main.ts).
It is the only process that holds the wallet's seed, and the service is the only one that holds the issuer's
secret. They talk over mutual TLS, and the service
[accepts only a server whose mesh identity is seal's](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/infrastructure/sponsor/https_sponsor_client.ts#L25-L36).
The sponsor also pays for what seal writes itself: deploying the registry, issuing credentials and placing the
approved predicates.

## Who may do what

- **A courier** — with identity's access token,
  [and only an access token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/infrastructure/identity/jose_token_verifier.adapter.ts#L50-L58)
  — asks for a credential and reads only its own.
- **Anyone**, with no account, reads the approved predicates, the registry snapshot and the phone's page, relays a
  presentation, and checks whether a challenge was answered. The gateway limits the relay to
  [20 requests a minute per address](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-api-gateway/blob/5c31fc4d53c22d0427c72491c2f72bb68c1b5ed7/config/gateway.pkl#L714-L730).
- **An admin** approves and withdraws predicates; anyone else is
  [refused](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/653588f8ab8bc77a1e1662df3b35e49e9ecc83cf/src/infrastructure/http/predicate_routes.ts#L173-L174).
  The gateway serves those two routes only to the staff network; see [the gateway](/architecture/gateway/).
