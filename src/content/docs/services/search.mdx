search answers "which products match these words" fast enough to keep up with a shopper who is still
typing. It owns nothing it answers about: catalog owns products, identity owns merchants, order owns
orders. search keeps a copy of each in Typesense, a search engine built for ranked, typo-tolerant text
search, follows each owner's change feed to keep the copy current, and can throw the copy away and build
it again from the owners at any time.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against search [v1.0.0](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/tree/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee),
the version in production.

## What it serves

Over HTTP, through the gateway,
[two routes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/httpapi/router.go#L20-L32),
and neither needs a token: `/api/v1/products/search` finds products and `/api/v1/products/suggest`
completes what a shopper is typing. What they return is what a shop window shows anyway — a product's
title, description, category, list price and the principal of the merchant selling it.

search serves the same two operations over gRPC, but nothing may call them. Its list of callers
[is empty unless one is named](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/config/config.go#L42),
production names none, and
[an empty list lets nobody through](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/grpcapi/authorization.go#L53-L59).

## Finding products

A search takes `q`, any number of `category`, a `sort` of `relevance`, `newest` or `price_asc`, and an
`offset` and `limit`.
[The query is checked before Typesense sees it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/search/query.go#L70-L84):
text longer than 256 bytes, or holding a control character, is `400 malformed_query`. A page holds 24
products unless the request asks otherwise, and
[never more than 100](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/search/query.go#L59-L68).

Typesense matches the words against
[the title, the category and the description, weighted four, two and one](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/typesense/schema.go#L26-L37),
puts an exact match first, and counts the matches
[by category, merchant and currency](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/typesense/schema.go#L39-L50),
so a shop can show filters with a number beside each. A product in any of the categories asked for
matches. Every value placed in a filter is
[quoted, with backticks removed first](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/typesense/query.go#L10-L39),
so a category cannot close the quote and add a clause of its own. Typesense pages by number rather than by
offset, so
[an offset is rounded down to the start of its page](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/typesense/query.go#L57-L63).

The price in a hit is catalog's list price as of the last sync. search moves no money: what a buyer pays is
worked out again at checkout, by [pricing](/services/pricing/), from what catalog holds at that moment.

## Suggestions

`suggest` takes what has been typed so far and answers
[up to ten distinct product titles](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/typesense/searcher.go#L108-L149),
or as many as `limit` asks for, up to a hundred. Nothing typed yet
[is answered with no suggestions](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/httpapi/router.go#L57-L62),
without asking Typesense.

## How fresh an answer is

Every search answer carries `index`: the time of the last change it includes, how old the index is, and
whether it is stale.
[The age is the time since the last sync pass that finished](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/querying/finder.go#L49-L60),
and an index is stale
[after ten minutes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/config/config.go#L45-L51).
A stale index still answers, with `200`: an older answer is more use to a shopper than none, and the
client decides what to show. A sync pass saves its place even when nothing changed, so a quiet shop does
not look stale — only a sync that has stopped working does.

## Keeping up with the owners

`syncd` runs every thirty seconds and, for each collection,
[reads the owner's change feed](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/indexing/syncer.go#L40-L102)
from where it last stopped — a cursor of the last change's time and id, kept in PostgreSQL — two hundred
records at a time. It writes what changed into the live index, removes what the owner reports gone — a
product catalog has taken off sale, for one — and only then saves the cursor. A page that fails leaves the
cursor where it was, so the next pass reads the same page again: the index can fall behind, but it does
not skip.
[The failure is logged](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/cmd/syncd/main.go#L108-L128)
with what it means: the cursor has not moved and the index is getting older.

A record search cannot turn into a document — a product with no usable SKU, an order with no buyer — is
[refused on its own](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/catalogclient/convert.go#L31-L52):
it is
[logged at `ERROR` with its id](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/indexing/refusals.go#L10-L30),
the rest of the page is written, and the cursor moves on. Failing the page instead would fail it again on
every pass, on the same record, and that collection would never move again.

## Rebuilding a collection

Each collection in Typesense is an alias pointing at a generation: a real collection named after the
second it was built, such as `products_v1760000000`. A rebuild
[builds a new generation from the start of the owner's feed](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/indexing/reindexer.go#L44-L144)
while searches keep reading the old one, then checks its work. The new generation of products must hold at
least as many documents as
[catalog counts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/catalogclient/client.go#L78-L87),
less those refused; merchants and orders, which no owner counts, as many as the rebuild sent. Only then
does it
[point the alias at the new generation](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/typesense/index.go#L114-L125).
A search never reads a half-built index, and a rebuild that came up short is not used.

Rebuilds are run on demand, for one collection or all three. A new installation has nothing to search
until the first one, and until then searchd reports that it is not ready.

### A sync and a rebuild never interleave

Both write a collection and both save its cursor. With nothing between them, a sync pass that began
before a rebuild was promoted could write into the old generation after the alias had moved — the removal
of a product taken off sale, say — and then save a cursor past the rebuild's, so the new generation would
never hear of it. So each collection has
[a PostgreSQL advisory lock](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/postgres/lock.go#L24-L56),
[taken inside a transaction](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/postgres/queries.sql#L21-L22).
A sync pass holds it for the whole pass; a rebuild holds it
[only while it promotes and saves its cursor](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/indexing/reindexer.go#L114-L126),
so a long rebuild does not stop the sync. The lock belongs to a transaction, so a process that dies while
holding it lets go with its session.

## Orders and merchants

search also indexes merchants and orders, but neither the gateway nor the gRPC surface serves them. The
code keeps orders private regardless:
[an order search must be made on behalf of a principal](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/querying/finder.go#L34-L42),
it
[matches only orders that principal bought or sold](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/typesense/query.go#L17-L24),
and
[orders are never suggested](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/querying/finder.go#L63-L71).

## When something is down

Every failure carries a kind, and the kind decides the answer:
[`400` for a malformed query, `503` when Typesense or PostgreSQL cannot answer, `500` for anything else](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/httpapi/status.go#L9-L24).
The body names the kind and nothing else. Typesense is given five seconds and each owner's feed ten.

## How the code is laid out

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `internal/search` | the domain: documents, cursors, queries, error kinds and the ports the use cases need — standard library only, which [`bin/check-core-purity`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/bin/check-core-purity) checks in CI |
| `internal/querying` | finding and suggesting, and how fresh an answer is |
| `internal/indexing` | the sync pass and the rebuild |
| `internal/typesense` | the engine: schemas, generations and the alias, queries |
| `internal/postgres` | the cursors and the collection lock, through sqlc |
| `internal/catalogclient`, `identityclient`, `orderclient` | the change feeds, each turning its owner's records into documents |
| `internal/httpapi`, `internal/grpcapi` | the two front doors |
| `internal/wiring` | the dependency graph, built with dig; [`bin/check-boundaries`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/bin/check-boundaries) keeps dig there and in `cmd/` |
| `cmd/` | four programs: `searchd` serves, `syncd` follows, `reindex` rebuilds, `migrate` changes the schema |

The four programs ship in one image, so the program answering searches and the one writing the index are
never different versions.

## Data

Typesense holds three collections — products, merchants and orders — each an alias over its live
generation. PostgreSQL holds
[one cursor per collection](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/migrations/00001_search_state.sql#L2-L10)
and when it was last saved. Nothing search stores is the truth: all of it can be rebuilt from the owners.

## Who may do what

- **Anyone** may search products and ask for suggestions, without a token: the results are what a shop
  window shows.
- **Nobody** may call the gRPC surface. Only health and reflection answer there, and only to a
  certificate from Kinetix's CA:
  [the handshake requires one, over TLS 1.3](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/grpcapi/credentials.go#L63-L68).
- **To read its sources**, search presents a certificate naming search. identity
  [lets it follow the merchant feed and make no other call](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/16fba71e46c89d6032b5ccc94a040af2fe97b1f1/src/infrastructure/mesh/peer_allow_list.ts#L48-L63),
  and order
  [lets it follow the order feed and make no other call](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/bf6483a1e8c5c21de00425bbdaa3ce30dec29450/Security/PeerAllowList.cs#L57-L71);
  see [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

- `searchd` serves both ports. `/health/live` answers when the process is up;
  [`/health/ready`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/wiring/http.go#L25-L46)
  pings PostgreSQL and asks Typesense which generation is live, so the container is ready only when there
  is something to search.
- `syncd` serves no port, and its log is how it is watched:
  [each page it applies](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/internal/indexing/syncer.go#L83-L96)
  is logged with how many records it wrote, removed and refused, and each failed pass says the cursor did
  not move. Both log JSON to standard output.
- On `SIGTERM`, searchd
  [stops gRPC gracefully and gives HTTP fifteen seconds](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/cmd/searchd/main.go#L96-L101)
  to finish. syncd stops at once; a page it had not finished is read again next time, because its cursor
  was never saved.
