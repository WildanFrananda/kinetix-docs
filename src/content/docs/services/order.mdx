order owns a customer's cart and every order from checkout until it is completed, cancelled or returned.
It owns no price, no stock and no money. At checkout it asks the services that do, records their answers,
and coordinates the steps that reserve and charge — so most of what order gets right is knowing which
facts are not its own.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against order [v1.5.1](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/tree/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab),
the version in production.

## What it serves over gRPC

Other services tell order what happened to an order — packed, delivered, returned — and read orders back,
[with the merchant who sold it and the courier who delivered it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Grpc/OrderGrpcServerService.cs#L88-L95)
— which is how review knows whom a review is about. Each row names who calls it, linked to the line that
does.

Order also serves [its lifecycle log](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Grpc/OrderLifecycleGrpcService.cs#L19-L49),
every change of every order, page by page, to the [notary](/services/notary/), which anchors it on chain. Every
status an order or a return reaches is
[written to the log in the same transaction as the change](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Lifecycle/LifecycleLog.cs#L13-L29),
with who did it: the buyer, the merchant, the courier, an admin or order itself.

Search follows orders through a change feed whose cursor is a database transaction, not a time. A cursor that
carries one is
[served in commit-safe order](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L694-L740): only orders written by
transactions older than every transaction still running. An order whose transaction commits late is served
late, and never skipped.

## How a checkout runs

A checkout is a sequence of questions, each to the service that owns the answer, and then a saga that
holds what was agreed:

1. **The cart** is read from Redis. The prices in it are not trusted: order asks catalog for each product
   again, because a cart can be fourteen days old — see
   [Never trust a number the client can type](/learn/trust-boundaries/). A cart that holds a product the buyer
   sells
   [is refused with `422`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L308-L316):
   a seller cannot buy from itself.
2. **The merchant** catalog names for the cart must still be allowed to sell: order
   [asks identity](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L71-L73)
   at every checkout, and refuses with `409` when it may not and `503` when identity cannot say.
3. **Shipping** is quoted for the parcel, on the route it travels.
   [The parcel's weight](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L318-L330) is catalog's weight for each
   product times its quantity. [The route](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L332-L347) runs from the
   point identity holds for the merchant's store to the address the buyer typed, placed by identity's
   geocoder. [Matching offers the courier options for that route and weight, and pricing prices them](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L499-L607).
   A product
   with no weight, a store with no point on the map, or an address that cannot be placed precisely enough
   [refuses the checkout with `422`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Controllers/OrderController.cs#L187-L214), and a geocoder that cannot
   answer refuses it with `503`. The order stores the drop-off point and the weight.
4. **The total** comes from pricing. If pricing cannot be asked, the checkout is refused with `503` rather than charged at a remembered price —
   see [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/). The voucher the order records is
   [the one pricing applied](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L101), not the code the buyer typed, so a voucher pricing refused is never
   redeemed. Each item is recorded at
   [the price pricing charged for that line](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L369-L406),
   flash sale included, and the checkout is refused if pricing left a line out or its lines do not add up
   to its subtotal — a return is refunded from these amounts, so they have to be what was paid. The total
   [must also split exactly](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L408-L438)
   into the merchant's share and the shipping fee, in whole cents, because that is how payment holds it, and
   [a total of zero is refused](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L135-L137):
   there is nothing to hold.
5. **The saga** then redeems the voucher, allocates flash-sale stock, reserves stock, holds the money in
   escrow and asks warehouse for a fulfillment task, recording each step before it calls and compensating
   newest-first when one is refused — see [The checkout saga](/learn/checkout-saga/).

An `Idempotency-Key` header makes a retried checkout return the first order instead of creating a second
— see [The same request, twice](/learn/idempotency/). The key belongs to the buyer, and
[a repeat is compared with the first request](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L246-L278):
the same key on a different checkout is refused with `422`, and a repeat that arrives while the first is still
running gets `409` and is asked to come back.

## When the merchant is paid

The buyer's money stays in payment's escrow until order says the order is complete. An order is
[completed](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Completion/OrderCompletion.cs#L100-L148)
once it has been delivered, its return window has closed — seven days from the time the courier reported
the delivery, a length order will not start without — and no return against it is still open or waiting for
its refund. Only then does order ask payment to release the escrow to the merchant.

Completing an order and opening a return both
[lock the order's row](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Completion/OrderCompletion.cs#L41)
before they read it, so a return cannot be opened against an order whose money is being paid out, and a
completion that waited behind a new return sees it. The order becomes `COMPLETED` first and the release
is then owed: if payment cannot be reached it is
[retried with backoff](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Completion/OrderCompletion.cs#L150-L173),
and if payment refuses, it stops and waits for a person rather than retrying forever. What order records about
a failed attempt is
[a fixed reason](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Faults/FailureReason.cs#L10-L17)
— which service, and whether it refused, was unreachable, timed out or failed — or the refusing service's own
words; the error itself goes to the log.

## Returns

A return is opened by the merchant through warehouse, and only against a delivered order; a completed
order's window has closed. When the goods come back, every line must
[name an item of the order and no more than was bought](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Returns/ReturnsHandler.cs#L222-L238).
The buyer is refunded
[the share of the merchant's money those goods were worth](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Returns/ReturnsHandler.cs#L202-L220)
— the order's total less its shipping fee, times the returned value over the value of everything bought,
rounded down to the cent — so a voucher is shared out with it, a full return refunds the merchant's whole
share, and the courier keeps the fee for a delivery that was made. The refund is paid from the escrow
under the key
[`return:{return number}`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Returns/ReturnRefunds.cs#L24),
so a retry cannot pay it twice.

## Sending a courier

When warehouse reports an order packed, order
[records that the order is owed a courier](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Fulfillment/FulfillmentPackedHandler.cs#L62-L81)
before it asks anyone anything, and then
[tries](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Fulfillment/CourierDispatcher.cs#L28-L47): it asks identity where the store is,
[asks matching for a courier](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Fulfillment/CourierDispatcher.cs#L49-L105) to the drop-off point
and for the weight stored at checkout, and gives warehouse the tracking number matching issued. An order
placed before 2026-10-18 has no stored drop-off; its courier goes to the point on the buyer's profile.
Whatever does not go through is retried, backing off to an hour — identity or the fleet out of reach, no
courier free yet, warehouse not told. A retry is safe because matching books one job per order number and
answers a repeat with the same courier and tracking number. The retry stops and waits for a person only when
matching says the job is already finished or warehouse refuses the number.

## How the code is laid out

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `Controllers/` | the HTTP surface: cart, checkout, orders, saga administration, health |
| `Application/` | the checkout saga, the order and cart services, delivery, completion and returns |
| `Application/Ports/` | the interfaces the application needs — [one per collaborator](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/tree/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Ports), such as `IPricingClient` and `IStockClient` |
| `Infrastructure/` | what implements those ports: gRPC clients and the gRPC server, EF Core persistence, the Redis cart store, background workers, settings, observability |
| `Domain/` | entities and enums, with no framework code |
| `Security/` | service identity on the mesh, caller allow lists, token validation |

The application depends on its ports, not on gRPC: the checkout saga is tested with fakes of
`IPricingClient` and `IStockClient`, while `Infrastructure/Grpc/` is the only place that knows a
collaborator is a remote service.

## Data

PostgreSQL, through EF Core, holds
[orders and their items, checkout sagas and their steps, compensation attempts, the shipping fees owed to
couriers, the couriers owed to packed orders, the escrow releases owed to merchants, returns, and the
lifecycle log](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Persistence/OrderDbContext.cs#L13-L23). The log
[refuses `UPDATE`, `DELETE` and `TRUNCATE`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Migrations/20261016134224_OrdersKeepALifecycleLog.cs#L62-L74),
and [a status written without its event fails to commit](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Migrations/20261016134224_OrdersKeepALifecycleLog.cs#L76-L127).

Redis holds carts, one per customer under
[`KinetixOrder:cart:principal:{principal id}`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Redis/RedisCartStore.cs#L11),
[expiring fourteen days after the last change](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/CartService.cs#L12).
A cart carries a version, and a change is
[written only if the version is still the one that was read](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Redis/RedisCartStore.cs#L16-L24),
so two edits at once cannot lose one. A paid checkout
[takes out of the cart only what it bought](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/CartService.cs#L115-L152):
a line added while it ran stays. A cart is the only thing order keeps outside its database, and nothing in it
is believed at checkout.

## Who may do what

- **Customers** reach the cart and orders through the gateway with an access token — only an access
  token, never the refresh or two-factor token signed by the same key; see
  [Tokens that can be taken back](/learn/tokens-that-can-be-taken-back/). Anyone may buy, except
  [their own products](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/CartService.cs#L25-L28),
  which cannot be put in a cart.
- **An order is read by its owner or by staff.**
  [Anyone else gets the same `404`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Controllers/OrderController.cs#L359-L371)
  as for an order that does not exist, so the answer does not reveal that it does.
- **Staff** — the `admin` role — also read the sagas and the returns that need attention. The gateway admits
  those routes only from Kinetix's staff network; see [the gateway](/architecture/gateway/).
- **An admin** may
  [complete a delivered order early](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Controllers/OrderCompletionController.cs#L19-L57),
  under the same rules except the window, and may
  [reject a return whose goods never came back](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Returns/ReturnRejection.cs#L18-L62),
  with a reason. Both are written to the lifecycle log under the admin's principal, and a token that names
  none is refused. A return does not lapse on a timer: the merchant both opens it and records the goods
  coming back, so a timer would pay a merchant for never recording them. A return whose goods came back
  cannot be rejected — the buyer is owed.
- **Every admin action is audited.** Completing an order, rejecting a return, reading the queues, and reading
  someone else's order are each
  [logged with the admin's principal, the target and the outcome](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Observability/AdminAudit.cs#L30-L42),
  and counted.
- **Warehouse's report that a parcel is packed** is accepted only for a paid order of the same
  merchant; an order that was cancelled or refunded is not revived and no courier is sent.
- **Other services** reach the gRPC port only with a certificate from Kinetix's CA naming a service on
  order's allow list, and
  [each is allowed only the calls it makes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Security/PeerAllowList.cs#L57-L71):
  review reads an order, matching reports a delivery, search follows the change feed, the notary reads the
  lifecycle log, and warehouse reports a packed parcel and a return — so no caller can report a delivery or a
  returned parcel unless that is its job. Order checks the identity of every server it calls in return; see
  [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

- `/health` answers when the process is up; `/health/ready`
  [runs a real query](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Controllers/HealthController.cs#L19-L38)
  and turns `503` while the instance drains after `SIGTERM`, so traffic stops before the process does.
- `/metrics` serves Prometheus text with the names the platform contract gives order.
- A database order cannot reach is
  [answered `503`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Http/UnhandledExceptionHandler.cs#L30-L52),
  and [`UNAVAILABLE` over gRPC](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Grpc/DatabaseOutageInterceptor.cs#L17-L22),
  never as a refusal. An unexpected error is a `500` with a trace id and no internal text.
- A token signed by a key order does not hold makes it
  [fetch identity's key set again at most every ten seconds](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Security/JwksKeyProvider.cs#L82-L102), one
  fetch at a time, so tokens with made-up key ids cannot make identity answer once per request. When that
  fetch fails, the request is
  [answered `503 IDENTITY_UNAVAILABLE`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Security/AccessTokenValidation.cs#L51-L70),
  never "invalid token".
- Every outbound gRPC call
  [has a deadline](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Http/GrpcDeadlineInterceptor.cs#L14-L23),
  [a setting order will not start without](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Infrastructure/Configuration/OrderSettings.cs#L30)
  — five seconds as Kinetix runs it — and placing an address on the map has a longer one of its own, so a
  dependency that hangs becomes an answer instead of a stuck request.
