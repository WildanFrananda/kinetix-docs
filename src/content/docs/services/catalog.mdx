catalog owns products and categories: what a product is called, which merchant sells it, which category
it is filed under, and its price. Order asks catalog what to charge rather than believing the cart, pricing
asks it who owns a product before letting a seller discount it, and search copies the whole catalogue from
it. Stock is not catalog's: it asks warehouse, and says so when warehouse cannot answer.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against catalog [v0.1.15](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/tree/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564),
the version in production.

## What it serves over gRPC

Order and pricing ask for one product by its SKU. Search walks the whole catalogue through a change feed
and counts what is on sale, to check that its copy is complete.

rather than leaving it out, which is how search learns to drop it.

## Who may list a product

A product is created, changed and withdrawn by the seller whose merchant sells it, and by nobody else:

- **The seller must be allowed to trade.** Before any write, catalog asks identity
  [which merchant the seller trades as and whether it may sell](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/application/services/product_service.py#L120-L131).
  If identity cannot be asked, the write is refused with `503` — an outage is not read as "this seller
  may" or "this seller may not"; see [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/).
- **The product must be theirs.** Changing or withdrawing a product
  [takes an exact match](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/application/services/product_service.py#L151-L192)
  between its merchant and the seller's.
- **A SKU names one product.** A new product is inserted and an existing one is
  [updated by its id](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/infrastructure/repositories/django_product_repository.py#L93-L115),
  never matched by SKU, so a SKU that belongs to another product is refused with `409` instead of
  reaching that product.
- **A product on sale has a merchant.** The database itself refuses an active product without one,
  through a [check constraint](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/infrastructure/models/product_model.py#L26-L34).

Withdrawing a product sets it inactive; it is never deleted, because orders, the change feed and the
warehouse all still name it by its SKU.

## What a product may say

[Every field is checked](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/application/services/product_field_parser.py)
before it is stored, and a request that fails is answered `400` naming the field:

- **The price** is more than zero, with at most two decimal places, and fits the column.
- **The currency** is the rupiah, the only currency order settles in. A price in anything else would be a
  listing nobody can buy.
- **The SKU** has no spaces or slashes, because it is part of a URL; the title and the image URL fit their
  columns, and the image URL is `http` or `https`.

A listing page holds
[at most a hundred products](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/application/services/product_filter_parser.py#L7),
because every product on it is a question to warehouse.

## Stock is warehouse's answer

Catalog shows stock but does not hold it. A listing asks warehouse once per merchant on the page, and when
warehouse cannot answer, the stock is
[reported as unknown](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/application/services/product_service.py#L44-L57)
— not as zero, which would tell a buyer something is sold out because a dependency was slow. A product's
stock is one of three answers: in stock, out of stock, or unknown.

## Categories

Categories are managed by an [`admin`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/api/views/category_view.py#L20-L33)
and read by anyone. A category that still holds products, withdrawn ones included,
[is not deleted](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/infrastructure/repositories/django_product_repository.py#L148-L157):
the request is answered `409` with how many products are in it. Deleting a category never deletes a product.

## How the code is laid out

catalog is hexagonal: the domain knows nothing of Django, and the outside world reaches it only through
ports. Under `core/`:

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `domain/` | entities, errors and the ports — `ProductRepository` for what catalog owns, `...ServicePort` for what another service owns |
| `application/` | the product and category services, and the parsers that check what a request may say |
| `infrastructure/` | Django models and the repository, the gRPC clients and server, mesh security, circuit breakers, observability |
| `api/` | thin Django REST Framework views and the wiring that builds the services |

The services are tested with fakes of every port and no database; the repository, the change feed and the
rules the database enforces are tested against PostgreSQL.

## Data

PostgreSQL holds products and categories. Products carry an index on `(updated_at, sku)`, which is what
the change feed walks: a cursor of the last timestamp and SKU it returned, so a page never skips or repeats
a product even when many change in the same instant.

## Who may do what

- **Anyone** reads the listing, a product by its SKU, and the categories.
- **A seller** — with an identity access token,
  [only an access token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/infrastructure/security/token_verifier.py#L64-L65),
  see [Tokens that can be taken back](/learn/tokens-that-can-be-taken-back/) — creates, changes and
  withdraws their own merchant's products, under the rules above.
- **An admin** manages categories.
- **Other services** reach the gRPC port only with a certificate from Kinetix's CA naming a service on
  catalog's allow list; see [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

The same image runs as two containers: one serves HTTP through gunicorn, the other serves gRPC.

- `/health` answers when the process is up; `/health/ready`
  [runs a real query](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/api/views/readiness_view.py#L11-L22).
  The gRPC container is probed over gRPC's own health protocol.
- `/metrics` serves Prometheus text with the names the platform contract gives catalog.
- Calls to identity and warehouse have a five-second timeout and sit behind
  [a circuit breaker](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/infrastructure/resilience/circuit_breaker.py#L23-L29)
  that opens after three
  [peer faults](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/infrastructure/resilience/is_peer_fault.py)
  in a row and tries again after fifteen seconds. A refusal is an answer, not a fault, and does not
  count towards it.
- On `SIGTERM` the gRPC server
  [reports itself not serving and drains](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2c14b3ad9d7f8de5df3e6eb584dd4e25a8eca564/core/management/commands/grpcserver.py#L91-L94)
  for up to ten seconds; gunicorn gives HTTP workers 25 seconds.
