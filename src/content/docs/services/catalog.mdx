catalog owns products and categories: what a product is called, which merchant sells it, which category
it is filed under, and its price. Order asks catalog what to charge rather than believing the cart, pricing
asks it who owns a product before letting a seller discount it, and search copies the whole catalogue from
it. Stock is not catalog's: it asks warehouse, and says so when warehouse cannot answer.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against catalog [v1.0.0](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/tree/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3),
the version in production.

## What it serves over gRPC

Order and pricing ask for one product by its SKU, and order reads its weight to price the shipping. Search
walks the whole catalogue through a change feed and counts what is on sale, to check that its copy is
complete.

<ServiceServes service="catalog" />

A product that has been withdrawn
[is not found](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/repositories/django_product_repository.py#L139-L144)
by `GetProduct`, so a cart that still holds one cannot be checked out. The change feed
[reports it as removed](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/repositories/django_product_repository.py#L108-L112)
rather than leaving it out, which is how search learns to drop it. The feed's cursor is the database
transaction that last changed a product:
[it serves only products written by transactions older than every transaction still running](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/repositories/django_product_repository.py#L77-L134),
so a product whose transaction commits late is served late and never skipped.

## Who may list a product

A product is created, changed and withdrawn by the seller whose merchant sells it, and by nobody else:

- **The seller must be allowed to trade.** Before any write, catalog asks identity
  [which merchant the seller trades as and whether it may sell](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/application/services/product_service.py#L122-L133).
  If identity cannot be asked, the write is refused with `503` — an outage is not read as "this seller
  may" or "this seller may not"; see [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/).
- **The product must be theirs.** Changing or withdrawing a product
  [takes an exact match](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/application/services/product_service.py#L154-L199)
  between its merchant and the seller's.
- **A SKU names one product.** A new product is inserted and an existing one is
  [updated by its id](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/repositories/django_product_repository.py#L153-L176),
  never matched by SKU, so a SKU that belongs to another product is refused with `409` instead of
  reaching that product.
- **A product on sale has a merchant, and a weight that is there is positive.** The database itself refuses
  an active product without a merchant, and a weight of zero or less, through
  [check constraints](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/models/product_model.py#L28-L34).

Withdrawing a product sets it inactive; it is never deleted, because orders, the change feed and the
warehouse all still name it by its SKU.

## What a product may say

[Every field is checked](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/application/services/product_field_parser.py)
before it is stored, and a request that fails is answered `400` naming the field:

- **The price** is more than zero, with at most two decimal places, and fits the column.
- **The currency** is the rupiah, the only currency order settles in. A price in anything else would be a
  listing nobody can buy.
- **The weight** is
  [a positive whole number of grams, at most 1,000,000](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/application/services/product_field_parser.py#L81-L96),
  and a new product must have one, because shipping is priced by weight. A product created before
  2026-10-18 may have none, and catalog does not invent one: order refuses to check it out until its seller
  sets it. `GetProduct` sends a weight only when there is one, so "no weight" is never read as zero.
- **The SKU** has no spaces or slashes, because it is part of a URL; the title and the image URL fit their
  columns, and the image URL is `http` or `https`.

A listing page holds
[at most a hundred products](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/application/services/product_filter_parser.py#L7),
because every product on it is a question to warehouse.

## Stock is warehouse's answer

Catalog shows stock but does not hold it. A listing asks warehouse once per merchant on the page, and when
warehouse cannot answer, the stock is
[reported as unknown](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/application/services/product_service.py#L44-L57)
— not as zero, which would tell a buyer something is sold out because a dependency was slow. A product's
stock is one of three answers: in stock, out of stock, or unknown.

## Categories

Categories are managed by an [`admin`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/api/views/category_view.py#L20-L33)
and read by anyone. A category that still holds products, withdrawn ones included,
[is not deleted](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/repositories/django_product_repository.py#L209-L218):
the request is answered `409` with how many products are in it. Deleting a category never deletes a product.

## How the code is laid out

catalog is hexagonal: the domain knows nothing of Django, and the outside world reaches it only through
ports. Under `core/`:

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `domain/` | entities, errors and the ports — `ProductRepository` for what catalog owns, `...ServicePort` for what another service owns |
| `application/` | the product and category services, and the parsers that check what a request may say |
| `infrastructure/` | Django models and the repository, the gRPC clients and server, mesh security, circuit breakers, observability |
| `api/` | thin Django REST Framework views and the wiring that builds the services |

The services are tested with fakes of every port and no database; the repository, the change feed and the
rules the database enforces are tested against PostgreSQL.

## Data

PostgreSQL holds products and categories. Every product carries
[the transaction that last changed it, stamped by a trigger on every insert and update](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/migrations/0009_products_record_their_change_transaction.py#L10-L33),
and an index on that transaction and the SKU, which is what the change feed walks: the cursor is the
transaction and SKU it returned last, so a page never skips or repeats a product, even when many change in
one transaction. A cursor without a transaction is still read the old way, by `(updated_at, sku)`; search no
longer sends one.

## Who may do what

- **Anyone** reads the listing, a product by its SKU, and the categories.
- **A seller** — with an identity access token,
  [only an access token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/security/token_verifier.py#L65-L66),
  see [Tokens that can be taken back](/learn/tokens-that-can-be-taken-back/) — creates, changes and
  withdraws their own merchant's products, under the rules above. A token signed by a key catalog does not
  hold makes it
  [fetch identity's key set again at most every ten seconds](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/security/signing_key_set.py#L50-L61),
  and when that fetch fails the request is
  [answered `503`, not "invalid token"](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/security/identity_token_authentication.py#L29-L46).
- **An admin** manages categories.
- **Other services** reach the gRPC port only with a certificate from Kinetix's CA naming a service on
  catalog's allow list; see [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

The same image runs as two containers: one serves HTTP through gunicorn, the other serves gRPC.

- `/health` answers when the process is up; `/health/ready`
  [runs a real query](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/api/views/readiness_view.py#L11-L22).
  The gRPC container is probed over gRPC's own health protocol.
- `/metrics` serves Prometheus text with the names the platform contract gives catalog.
- Calls to identity and warehouse have a five-second timeout and sit behind
  [a circuit breaker](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/resilience/circuit_breaker.py#L23-L29)
  that opens after three
  [peer faults](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/infrastructure/resilience/is_peer_fault.py)
  in a row and tries again after fifteen seconds. A refusal is an answer, not a fault, and does not
  count towards it.
- On `SIGTERM` the gRPC server
  [reports itself not serving and drains](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/2a395740eb832a69bfd72c82be54779d421ddef3/core/management/commands/grpcserver.py#L91-L94)
  for up to ten seconds; gunicorn gives HTTP workers 25 seconds.
