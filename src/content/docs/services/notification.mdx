notification turns "order 42 was packed" into a push to the buyer's phone and an email in their inbox. A
service asks for one of six templates, names the principal it is for and supplies the facts the words
need; notification finds where that principal can be reached, writes down that it accepted the request,
and tries each channel. It owns two things: the device tokens that say which handset is logged in as
whom, and the record of what it was asked to send and what became of each attempt.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against notification [v1.0.2](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/tree/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23),
the version in production. In production no service is granted a template yet, so nothing is sent; the
handset route is live.

## What it serves

Over HTTP, through the gateway,
[one route with two methods](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-api-gateway/blob/d58e93dc52d6e6dc5d48314800442983ee80df40/config/gateway.pkl#L309-L330):
`POST /api/v1/notifications/devices` registers the calling handset for push and `DELETE` forgets it. Both
need an access token, which the gateway checks and notification checks again.

Over gRPC, four operations: `Notify` sends, `GetDelivery` reports what became of a notification, and
`RegisterDevice` and `ForgetDevice` let a trusted service act for a handset. Each caller is granted
operations one by one, and production grants none.

## What it calls

identity's `GetUserProfile`, for the email address of the principal a message is for, and two providers
outside the estate: Firebase Cloud Messaging for push, through
[its HTTP v1 API](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/HttpPushSender.scala#L31-L52),
and Resend for email,
[one JSON request per message](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/HttpEmailSender.scala#L32-L50).

## Sending a notification

A `Notify` request names a template, the recipient's principal, the template's parameters, optionally
the channels to use, and optionally an idempotency key.
[The order of the checks](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/application/Notify.scala#L22-L37)
is the order of what they protect:

1. **The template must be granted to the caller.** A service allowed to call `Notify` may still send only
   the templates named for it, so the service that dispatches couriers cannot tell a buyer their order
   was paid. A caller with no template grant sends nothing: `TEMPLATE_NOT_GRANTED`.
2. **The words must be complete.** The copy lives in
   [a catalogue](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/resources/messages/id.json),
   and every `{name}` in a title or body
   [must arrive as a parameter](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/domain/Message.scala#L23-L44).
   A missing one is refused as `TEMPLATE_PARAM_MISSING` rather than sent with a hole in it. A catalogue
   that lacks a template
   [stops the service starting](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/domain/MessageCatalogue.scala#L7-L11).
3. **A repeated key is a replay, not a second message.** Keys belong to the service that sent them. The
   same key with the same recipient, template and parameters
   [answers with the notification it made the first time](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/application/Notify.scala#L42-L49);
   the same key with anything else is `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`, never somebody else's notification.
4. **Someone must be reachable.** identity supplies the email address and notification's own registry
   the device tokens. A principal with no usable address on any channel is `NO_CHANNEL_AVAILABLE`, and
   leaves no record: a notification to nobody is not a notification.

Then the record is written,
[before anything is sent](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/application/Notify.scala#L68-L85).
A crash between the two leaves a notification that looks unsent, which somebody can act on, rather than
a message nobody has a trace of. Two first calls with one key can arrive together: the insert
[does nothing when the key is already taken](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/persistence/NotificationQueries.scala#L34-L48),
and the call that lost
[reads the winner and answers with it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/application/Notify.scala#L87-L106),
so exactly one message goes out.

Each channel is then
[tried and its outcome recorded](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/application/Notify.scala#L125-L153).
A provider that did not answer, or answered with a server error, is retried
[up to three times, waiting 200 ms and then 600 ms](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/retry/ExponentialBackoffRetry.scala#L23-L38).
A provider that refused — a dead token, a rejected address — is not: that is an answer, and asking again
gets the same one. Only
[failures that are not answers](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/domain/NotificationError.scala#L39-L43)
are transient. A failed channel does not fail the request: the notification was accepted, and its record
says which channels reached the recipient and why the others did not.

## A handset registers itself

A phone has no certificate from Kinetix's CA and reaches the estate only through the gateway, so device
registration is the one piece of business on notification's HTTP port.
[Every request needs a bearer token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/DeviceApi.scala#L56-L71),
verified against identity's published keys with
[RS256 only, identity's issuer, the estate's audience and `token_use: access`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/NimbusAccessTokenVerifier.scala#L31-L49).
An unsigned token, an HMAC token keyed with the public key and a refresh token are all refused.

The handset is registered to
[the principal the token names](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/DeviceApi.scala#L28-L44),
never to one the body claims. The body is read
[up to 4 KiB and no further](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/DeviceApi.scala#L73-L83),
and its platform must be
[`ANDROID` or `IOS`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/DeviceApi.scala#L85-L88).
A token another principal held
[moves to whoever registers it now](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/persistence/PostgresDeviceRegistry.scala#L25-L54):
it is the same phone, signed in as somebody else. Forgetting removes the token
[only if it belongs to the caller](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/persistence/PostgresDeviceRegistry.scala#L59-L67),
and answers `204` either way, so it says nothing about whose token it was.

When identity's keys cannot be fetched, the answer is `503 TOKEN_UNVERIFIABLE`, not `401`. "Your token is
bad" and "the keys could not be read" are different facts, and reporting the second as the first sends a
signed-in user to sign in again.

## Asking what happened

`GetDelivery` returns a notification's recipient, template and the outcome on each channel —
[and only to the service that sent it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/persistence/NotificationQueries.scala#L10-L16).
Another service is told it does not exist. When the store cannot be reached the call
[fails with `UNAVAILABLE`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/grpc/GrpcNotificationServer.scala#L30-L40)
rather than answering `found = false` for a notification that may well exist.

## How the code is laid out

notification is Scala 3 with Cats Effect, written tagless-final behind a hexagonal boundary.

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `domain/` | values, the error type, templates and grants, and the ports the use cases need — no effect type, no gRPC or HTTP |
| `application/` | the use cases — notify, report a delivery, register and forget a device — generic in their effect and tested against hand-written fakes |
| `infrastructure/grpc` | the gRPC server, the caller check, the identity client |
| `infrastructure/http` | the handset routes, token verification, the two providers, health and metrics |
| `infrastructure/persistence` | PostgreSQL through doobie, and the schema migration |
| `Main.scala` | the only place adapters are built and wired, by hand, with no container |

The compiler holds the rules: warnings are errors, and an unused import or a discarded value stops the
build. `Migrate` is a second entry point in the same image, so the schema and the service that reads it
are never different versions.

## Data

PostgreSQL, three tables:
[`notifications`, `notification_attempts` and `notification_devices`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/resources/db/migration/V1__notification_tables.sql#L1-L36).
A notification carries its sender, recipient, template and parameters; an idempotency key is
[unique per sender](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/resources/db/migration/V2__notifications_belong_to_their_sender.sql#L1-L17),
and the table refuses a template the service does not know. An attempt is one row per channel, with its
status, count and last error. A device token belongs to one principal at a time. A row the service cannot
read is reported as a store failure, never read as a guess.

## Who may do what

- **A handset** may register itself and forget its own token, with an access token from identity.
- **A service** may call only the gRPC operations granted to it by name, and send only the templates
  granted to it. Grants are written per operation —
  [`peer@package.Service/Method`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/grpc/PeerAllowList.scala#L10-L19),
  as order and identity read theirs — and the caller is
  [the service its certificate names](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/grpc/PeerAuthorizationInterceptor.scala#L19-L44),
  which reaches the handler
  [after whatever the client sent under that name is thrown away](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/grpc/CallerHeader.scala#L12-L14).
  Both lists
  [must be set even when empty](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/config/Env.scala#L12-L21),
  and a grant for an operation notification does not serve
  [stops it starting](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/Main.scala#L199-L211):
  a misspelt grant would otherwise grant nothing, silently. In production both are empty.
- **Any service with a certificate from Kinetix's CA** may ask the gRPC port what it serves, through
  reflection, and nothing else.
  [The handshake requires a client certificate](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/ServiceIdentity.scala#L10-L21).
- **To read an email address**, notification presents a certificate naming notification. identity
  [lets it read a profile and make no other call](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/16fba71e46c89d6032b5ccc94a040af2fe97b1f1/src/infrastructure/mesh/peer_allow_list.ts#L48-L63);
  see [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

- `/health` answers while the process runs and touches nothing.
  [`/health/ready`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/http/HttpApi.scala#L28-L43)
  asks PostgreSQL a real question, and
  [logs why it failed](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/infrastructure/persistence/PostgresReadiness.scala#L9-L21)
  rather than putting the driver's words in the response.
- `/metrics` serves the estate's metric names, and
  [every series starts at zero](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/Main.scala#L150-L154)
  when the process starts, so "nothing has failed yet" and "this does not report failures" never look
  the same.
- At start-up the log
  [names who is granted what](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notification-service/blob/8e4a1c51f9758b7acffa1bb27a97a35c48feaf23/src/main/scala/com/kinetix/notification/Main.scala#L133-L138);
  with nothing granted it reads `gRPC callers: nobody; templates: nobody`. A refused call is logged with
  the method and the peer.
- The schema changes in a one-shot `Migrate` run before the service starts, never from inside it, so
  replicas do not race to change it and a failed migration does not look like a service that would not
  boot.
