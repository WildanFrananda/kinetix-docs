matching runs the fleet. It knows each courier's vehicle and capacity, where every online courier is right
now, and every delivery job from the moment a packed order is handed to it until the parcel is delivered or
the job is cancelled. It owns no address and no price: order gives it the store's and the buyer's points,
pricing prices the journey it quotes, and order decides what a delivery means for the money.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against matching [v1.0.0](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/tree/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83),
the version in production.

## What it serves over gRPC

order asks matching for the shipping options of a cart and, once warehouse has packed an order, for a
courier. identity files a new courier's vehicle and activates the driver when an admin approves the account.

## Quoting a journey

`EstimateShippingOptions` measures the distance between the store and the buyer and
[answers which service tiers can carry the parcel](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/shipping.ex#L16-L32)
and why the others cannot: instant up to 15 km and 10 kg, same day up to 30 km and 20 kg, regular up to
500 km and 30 kg, and cargo for parcels of 10 kg or more. matching does not price a tier; the buyer picks
one, and pricing turns it into the fee — see [pricing](/services/pricing/).

## Choosing a courier

1. **One job per order.** order asks `DispatchCourier` with the order number and the two points, and matching
   [books a job for that order number, or finds the one it already booked](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L78-L83)
   — so a retried dispatch answers with the same job, courier and tracking number instead of booking a
   second one.
2. **The nearest courier who can carry it.** A pending job is
   [offered to the online couriers within three kilometres of the store](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L294-L302)
   whose vehicle can carry the parcel's weight, nearest first. Each courier is a process that holds their
   own state, and taking a job is an atomic claim on that process, so a courier is never handed more jobs
   at once than they may carry — one, unless their limit is raised.
3. **No courier free is not a refusal for good.** The job stays pending, and when a courier comes online
   [matching offers it the pending jobs again](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch/re_dispatcher.ex#L91-L95).
   order keeps asking until a courier is assigned, which is how warehouse receives the tracking number —
   see [order's courier dispatch](/services/order/#sending-a-courier).

## Delivering a parcel

A job moves
[from assigned to picked up to delivered](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L28-L32),
and only
[the courier it is assigned to](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L362-L363)
moves it; an admin can cancel it.

A delivery needs a photo. Once the parcel is picked up, the courier's app asks for
[an upload address](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L210-L218):
a URL, valid for ten minutes, that lets the phone put one photo into a private bucket under that job's
name. The app uploads the photo and sends its address with the delivery, and matching
[accepts the delivery only if the photo is that job's and is really there](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L263-L279).
If the bucket cannot be asked, the delivery is not refused and not accepted: the courier tries again. The
bucket is never public; the backoffice sees a photo through an address that expires in fifteen minutes.

## Telling order

order decides what a delivery means — the courier's fee, the start of the return window, when the merchant
is paid — so matching's part is to make sure order hears of it. A delivery is recorded with the time the
parcel arrived and
[reported at once](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch/delivery_report.ex#L25-L38);
if order cannot be reached, the report is
[owed and retried every minute](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch/delivery_report.ex#L41-L51),
backing off to an hour, always with the original delivery time. order records a delivery once and answers a
repeat as already recorded, so retrying is safe. Only
[order's own refusal stops the retrying](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/clients/order_grpc.ex#L100-L110);
a call that could not be made is unknown, not a refusal — see [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/).

## How the code is laid out

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch/` | delivery jobs, their rules, re-offering pending jobs, and reporting deliveries to order |
| `lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/` | couriers and their vehicles, one process per online courier, live positions and their persistence |
| `lib/fleet_pulse/shipping.ex` | the journey quote |
| `lib/fleet_pulse/storage/` | the proof-of-delivery bucket and the presigned addresses for it |
| `lib/fleet_pulse/servers/` | the gRPC servers order and identity call |
| `lib/fleet_pulse_web/` | the HTTP routes, the courier's and the merchant's websocket channels, and the plugs that authenticate them |

Contexts own the rules; the web layer and the gRPC servers only translate requests into calls on them.

## Data

PostgreSQL, through Ecto, holds couriers and their vehicles, delivery jobs with their proof of delivery and
whether order has heard of them, and the trail of position pings. The position the fleet works from lives in
memory, one process per online courier;
[pings are written to PostgreSQL every thirty seconds](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/persistence_batcher.ex#L44)
and [kept for thirty days](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/ping_retention.ex#L23-L24).
Photos of delivered parcels are kept in object storage, in a bucket of their own that only matching may
write.

## Who may do what

- **A courier** connects to the fleet's websocket with an identity access token for
  [an approved courier account with a vehicle in the fleet](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_socket.ex#L26-L34),
  joins only
  [their own driver channel](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L152-L153),
  and moves only the jobs assigned to them.
- **A seller** connects to the merchant channel for their own merchant only, and
  [hears about their own jobs and nobody else's](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/merchant_channel.ex#L41-L47).
- **The fleet's positions and the dispatch board** —
  [every courier's live position, the jobs, assigning and cancelling them](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse_web/router.ex#L24-L45)
  — are for the `admin` role. A courier's position is personal data; no other role reads it.
- **Other services** reach the gRPC port only with a certificate from Kinetix's CA naming a service on
  matching's allow list, and an entry can be narrowed to one gRPC service:
  [identity may file and activate couriers and nothing else](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/security/peer_authorization_interceptor.ex#L43-L55),
  so it cannot dispatch a courier or quote a journey; see [Who is calling?](/learn/who-is-calling/).

## Operating it

- `/health` answers when the process is up; `/health/ready`
  [runs a real query](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse_web/controllers/health_controller.ex#L31-L54),
  so the container is healthy only when the service can serve.
- `/metrics` serves Prometheus text with the names the platform contract gives matching.
- A courier whose app goes quiet keeps their process for a while, so a reconnect finds its last position;
  [an offline courier silent for fifteen minutes is stopped](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/idle_reaper.ex#L32),
  so a courier who never comes back does not keep a process forever.
- On `SIGTERM` the gRPC listener stops taking calls and
  [waits for the ones in flight](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7697467eac748c9090659622fcb398cab7004c83/lib/fleet_pulse/grpc_drain.ex#L1-L4)
  before the process ends.
