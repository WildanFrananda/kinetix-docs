notary makes two kinds of commitment on a public chain. The first is what the platform owes: hourly by
default, it takes payment's snapshot of each holder's balance, commits to the whole set and its total, and anchors the
commitment so that each holder can prove their own balance is inside it. The second is what happened: it
reads the append-only logs of order, matching and review, commits to every record with a salt of its own,
and anchors a batch per log. It
[owns no balance, no order and no review, and never moves money](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/CLAUDE.md#L12-L20):
it reads what the owner wrote and commits to it.

What the commitments prove, and how to check one in this browser, is in the Verify section:
[What a proof of liabilities proves](/verify/what-the-proof-proves/),
[What the evidence proves](/verify/what-the-evidence-proves/), and the two verifiers. This page is the
service behind them.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked
against notary [v1.5.0](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/tree/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c),

## What it serves

Over HTTP, through the gateway,
[four routes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/httpapi/router.go#L20-L25),
all behind an identity access token:

| Route | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/proofs/liabilities/{epoch}` | the caller's own proofs of liabilities for an epoch, or the latest |
| `POST /api/v1/proofs/registrations` | registers the address from which a holder will ask the chain for a statement |
| `GET /api/v1/proofs/evidence/orders/{order}` | the proofs of an order's lifecycle and delivery the caller is party to |
| `GET /api/v1/proofs/evidence/reviews/{review}` | the proof of a review the caller is party to |

Over gRPC, notary serves one operation, and only to itself: `ApproveEpoch`, between its two running
processes, described [below](#two-processes-and-two-tools). Traffic inside one service is not drawn on the
system map, so it is not in the tables on this page.

## What it calls

notary reads from four services and writes to one chain. It reads payment's liability snapshot and payment's
own record of it, and the three evidence logs. On the chain it sends its anchors, reads the statement
requests holders make, and calls the contracts' views.

## Two processes and two tools

One image carries
[five programs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/Dockerfile#L28-L52).
Production runs three of them:

- **notaryd**, the service. It
  [anchors liabilities and evidence, answers statement requests, and serves the proof API](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/cmd/notaryd/main.go#L36-L61).
- **notary-watch**, the council's second member. It
  [approves an epoch only after checking it itself](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/cmd/notary-watch/main.go#L82-L122),
  keeps no database, and signs off chain: it never sends a transaction.
- **notary-migrate**, which applies the schema once and exits.

The other two are tools for people outside Kinetix:
- **notary-verify**, the auditor's command line, checks a proof against the chain. Its browser build is
  what runs on this site's Verify pages.
- **notary-sponsor**, the holder's command line, takes a holder's case to the chain's courts, paid for by
  notary's paymaster.

## Anchoring what is owed

Every hour by default
([`defaultEpochInterval`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/config/daemon.go#L43)),
notaryd builds an epoch:

1. It [asks payment for a snapshot](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/anchoring/anchorer.go#L150-L164)
   of what is owed to each holder. If payment cannot answer, no epoch is built, and the next tick tries again.
2. It commits to every balance with a salt of its own, builds a sum tree whose root carries the total, and
   stores the epoch.
3. It asks notary-watch to approve it. The watch does not take notaryd's word. It
   [asks payment for payment's own record of that snapshot](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/approving/approver.go#L152-L246)
   and compares the count and the total, checks the ledger's next epoch and head, and rebuilds the tree
   itself before it signs.
4. With both signatures, sorted by signer, notaryd sends the anchor to the `AnchorCouncil`, the ledger's only
   anchorer, which
   [refuses fewer approvals than its threshold, a signer that is not a member, or approvals out of order](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/contracts/src/council/AnchorCouncil.sol#L141-L168).

A refusal from the watch
[abandons the epoch](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/anchoring/anchorer.go#L244-L251).
Any other failure is retried. So a commitment reaches the chain only when two parties have checked it
against payment independently, and the chain itself enforces that.

## Anchoring what happened

notaryd reads three append-only logs, each owned by the service that wrote it: order's lifecycle log,
matching's delivery evidence, and review's evidence log. Each record gets a salt of its own and becomes a
leaf. A batch per log closes every 24 hours by default
([`defaultEvidenceBatch`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/config/daemon.go#L46)),
and
[only one batch is in flight across the three](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/evidencing/closer.go#L42-L60).
Batches go to the `EvidenceRegistry`, under
[a key that must not be the liabilities anchorer's](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/evidencing/registry_check.go#L46-L70).

What notary keeps of a delivery or a review is the record's fields and the hashes the owner logged: the
photo's and the signature's SHA-256, the comment's SHA-256. It never sees the photo or the comment.

## Who may ask for what

- **A proof** needs an identity access token:
  [RS256, from identity's issuer for this audience, unexpired, and an access token, not a refresh token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/tokens/authenticator.go#L67-L105).
  The token's subject is the caller.
- **Liabilities**: a holder gets
  [their own accounts only, and only from an epoch that is final](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/proving/prover.go#L121-L183)
  on chain.
- **An order**:
  [the buyer, the merchant and whoever made a change get its lifecycle records; the courier and the order's parties get its delivery record](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/evidencing/prover.go#L19-L56).
- **A review**:
  [its author and the merchant, and the courier when it is a courier rating](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/notary/review_record.go#L26-L32).
- A record not yet anchored, or anchored and not yet final, comes back
  [counted as pending, not as absent](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/evidencing/prover.go#L81-L134).
- **`ApproveEpoch`**: notary-watch demands a client certificate and
  [answers the notary's identity and no other](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/wiring/watch_servers.go#L19).
- **The three evidence logs** are each opened to the notary alone by the service that owns them; see
  [review's evidence log](/services/review/#the-evidence-log).

## When something is down

- A feed that cannot be reached is
  [told apart from one that refused](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/feeds/connection.go#L40-L47).
  One failing log does not stop the other two, and a log's cursor moves only after its page is stored.
- The proof API answers a chain it cannot reach with
  [`503 chain_unavailable`, a store it cannot reach with `503 unavailable`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/httpapi/proofs.go#L122-L145),
  and an epoch that is not anchored with `404`. An outage is never an epoch that does not exist.
- The verifier has
  [an exit status for each verdict](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/verifycli/exit.go#L3-L18):
  a proof that is rejected, one that cannot be decided because the chain did not answer, and one whose
  ledger has moved to a successor are three different answers.

This is the rule [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/) describes, applied to commitments.

## How the code is laid out

Ports and adapters, with the names of what each package provides
([the layout rule](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/CLAUDE.md#L174-L193)):

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `internal/notary` | the domain and its ports, importing nothing but the standard library |
| `internal/anchoring`, `internal/evidencing`, `internal/approving`, `internal/proving`, `internal/answering` | the use cases |
| `internal/ethereum` | the only package that touches a chain |
| `internal/payment`, `internal/feeds`, `internal/epochwatch` | the only packages that speak gRPC |
| `internal/postgres` | the only package that touches the database |
| `internal/wiring`, `cmd/` | the composition roots, the only code that uses dig |
| `contracts/` | the Solidity contracts and their Foundry tests |

## Data

PostgreSQL, through pgx and code that sqlc generates. Epochs and their accounts, every anchor attempt,
holders' registrations, the three logs' cursors, evidence batches with their leaves, and copies of the
committed fields of each lifecycle, delivery and review record. A
[single-row lease](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/migrations/00001_notary.sql#L2-L7)
lets one writer anchor at a time. notary-watch keeps no database.

## Operating it

- Both running processes serve `/health/live`, `/health/ready` and `/metrics`.
- notaryd refuses to start against a chain or a contract it was not configured for. Before it serves
  anything it
  [checks the ledger, the council, the courts, the directory, the paymaster and the registry](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/cmd/notaryd/main.go#L110-L142),
  starting with
  [the chain id and the ledger's code](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/b5d5e1138dcde472ee90c48c8ed3fc315e9c8a2c/internal/ethereum/ledger.go#L48-L80).
- The chain is public, so anyone can watch notary work: every anchor is a transaction to the addresses
  above.
