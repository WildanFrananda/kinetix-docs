notary makes two kinds of commitment on a public chain. The first is what the platform owes: hourly by
default, it takes payment's snapshot of each holder's balance, commits to the whole set and its total, and anchors the
commitment so that each holder can prove their own balance is inside it. Since 2026-10-20 the total itself is no
longer published: a zero-knowledge proof, checked on chain, shows that the hidden total is the sum of the balances.
The second is what happened: it
reads the append-only logs of order, matching and review, commits to every record with a salt of its own,
and anchors a batch per log. It
[owns no balance, no order and no review, and never moves money](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/CLAUDE.md#L12-L20):
it reads what the owner wrote and commits to it.

What the commitments prove, and how to check one in this browser, is in the Verify section:
[What a proof of liabilities proves](/verify/what-the-proof-proves/),
[What the evidence proves](/verify/what-the-evidence-proves/), and the two verifiers. This page is the
service behind them.

The tables on this page are generated from the same verified data as the
[system map](/architecture/system-map/), at the commits linked from each row. The prose is checked

## At a glance

<ServiceFacts service="notary" />

notary is the one service that shares a language: Go, like search, because its domain needs go-ethereum.
Its contracts are Solidity, built and tested with Foundry. Its zero-knowledge proofs are made with gnark.

## What it serves

Over HTTP, through the gateway,
[four routes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/httpapi/router.go#L20-L25),
all behind an identity access token:

| Route | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/proofs/liabilities/{epoch}` | the caller's own proofs of liabilities for an epoch, or the latest |
| `POST /api/v1/proofs/registrations` | registers the address from which a holder will ask the chain for a statement |
| `GET /api/v1/proofs/evidence/orders/{order}` | the proofs of an order's lifecycle and delivery the caller is party to |
| `GET /api/v1/proofs/evidence/reviews/{review}` | the proof of a review the caller is party to |

Over gRPC, notary serves one operation, and only to itself: `ApproveEpoch`, between its two long-running
processes, described [below](#its-processes-and-tools). Traffic inside one service is not drawn on the
system map, so it is not in the tables on this page.

## What it calls

notary reads from four services and writes to one chain. It reads payment's liability snapshot and payment's
own record of it, and the three evidence logs. On the chain it sends its proofs and anchors, reads the
statement requests holders make, and calls the contracts' views.

## Its processes and tools

One image carries
[six programs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/Dockerfile#L28-L52).
Production runs four of them:

- **notaryd**, the service. It
  [anchors liabilities and evidence, answers statement requests, and serves the proof API](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/cmd/notaryd/main.go#L36-L61).
- **notary-prove**, which notaryd
  [starts as a child process for each epoch](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/proverproc/client.go#L29-L80).
  It reads the epoch's accounts on its standard input, writes the zero-knowledge proof on its standard
  output, and exits, so the prover's memory is held only while it proves.
- **notary-watch**, the council's second member. It
  [approves an epoch only after checking it itself](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/cmd/notary-watch/main.go#L82-L122),
  keeps no database, and signs off chain: it never sends a transaction.
- **notary-migrate**, which applies the schema once and exits.

The other two are tools for people outside Kinetix:
- **notary-verify**, the auditor's command line, checks a proof against the chain. Its browser build is
  what runs on this site's Verify pages.
- **notary-sponsor**, the holder's command line, takes a holder's case to the chain's courts, paid for by
  notary's paymaster.

## Anchoring what is owed

Every hour by default
([`defaultEpochInterval`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/config/daemon.go#L46)),
notaryd builds an epoch:

1. It [asks payment for a snapshot](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/anchoring/anchorer.go#L146-L160)
   of what is owed to each holder. If payment cannot answer, no epoch is built, and the next tick tries again.
2. It commits to every balance with a salt of its own and builds a Poseidon2 tree of depth 8, with a
   commitment to the total under a fresh blinding. notary-prove proves the tree in zero knowledge, and notaryd
   sends the proof to the ledger, which
   [verifies it on chain and records the epoch as proven](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/confidential/LiabilityLedgerV2.sol#L75-L115).
   The first generation, up to epoch 182, built a sum tree whose root carried the total, and needed no proof.
3. It asks notary-watch to approve it. The watch does not take notaryd's word. It
   [asks payment for payment's own record of that snapshot](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/approving/approver.go#L158-L258)
   and compares the count and the total, and checks the ledger's next epoch and head. It
   [rebuilds the tree's root and the total's commitment itself](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/approving/approver.go#L326-L363),
   from payment's rows and the salts and blinding notaryd sends, and
   [signs only once the ledger reports the proof passed](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/approving/approver.go#L260-L286).
4. With both signatures, sorted by signer, notaryd sends the anchor to the `ConfidentialAnchorCouncil`, the
   ledger's only anchorer, which
   [refuses fewer approvals than its threshold, a signer that is not a member, or approvals out of order](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/confidential/ConfidentialAnchorCouncil.sol#L142-L169).
   The ledger then
   [anchors the epoch only if it was proven](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/confidential/LiabilityLedgerV2.sol#L117-L166).

A refusal from the watch
[abandons the epoch](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/anchoring/anchorer.go#L370-L377).
Any other failure is retried. So a commitment reaches the chain only when two parties have checked it
against payment independently, and the chain itself enforces that.

## Anchoring what happened

notaryd reads three append-only logs, each owned by the service that wrote it: order's lifecycle log,
matching's delivery evidence, and review's evidence log. Each record gets a salt of its own and becomes a
leaf. A batch per log closes every 24 hours by default
([`defaultEvidenceBatch`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/config/daemon.go#L49)),
and
[only one batch is in flight across the three](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/evidencing/closer.go#L42-L60).
Batches go to the `EvidenceRegistry`, under
[a key that must not be the liabilities anchorer's](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/evidencing/registry_check.go#L46-L70).

What notary keeps of a delivery or a review is the record's fields and the hashes the owner logged: the
photo's and the signature's SHA-256, the comment's SHA-256. It never sees the photo or the comment.

## Who may ask for what

- **A proof** needs an identity access token:
  [RS256, from identity's issuer for this audience, unexpired, and an access token, not a refresh token](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/tokens/authenticator.go#L67-L105).
  The token's subject is the caller.
- **Liabilities**: a holder gets
  [their own accounts only, and only from an epoch that is final](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/proving/prover.go#L165-L237)
  on chain.
- **An order**:
  [the buyer, the merchant and whoever made a change get its lifecycle records; the courier and the order's parties get its delivery record](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/evidencing/prover.go#L19-L56).
- **A review**:
  [its author and the merchant, and the courier when it is a courier rating](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/notary/review_record.go#L26-L32).
- A record not yet anchored, or anchored and not yet final, comes back
  [counted as pending, not as absent](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/evidencing/prover.go#L81-L134).
- **`ApproveEpoch`**: notary-watch demands a client certificate and
  [answers the notary's identity and no other](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/wiring/watch_servers.go#L19).
- **The three evidence logs** are each opened to the notary alone by the service that owns them; see
  [review's evidence log](/services/review/#the-evidence-log).

## When something is down

- A feed that cannot be reached is
  [told apart from one that refused](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/feeds/connection.go#L40-L47).
  One failing log does not stop the other two, and a log's cursor moves only after its page is stored.
- The proof API answers a chain it cannot reach with
  [`503 chain_unavailable`, a store it cannot reach with `503 unavailable`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/httpapi/proofs.go#L127-L150),
  and an epoch that is not anchored with `404`. An outage is never an epoch that does not exist.
- The verifier has
  [an exit status for each verdict](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/verifycli/exit.go#L3-L18):
  a proof that is rejected, one that cannot be decided because the chain did not answer, and one whose
  ledger has moved to a successor are three different answers.

This is the rule [Unknown is not no](/learn/unknown-is-not-no/) describes, applied to commitments.

## How the code is laid out

Ports and adapters, with the names of what each package provides
([the layout rule](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/CLAUDE.md#L199-L221)):

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `internal/notary` | the domain and its ports, importing nothing but the standard library |
| `internal/anchoring`, `internal/evidencing`, `internal/approving`, `internal/proving`, `internal/answering` | the use cases |
| `internal/poseidon`, `internal/plonk` | the second generation's Poseidon2 tree, and its circuit, prover and the Solidity verifier gnark exports |
| `internal/ethereum` | the only package that touches a chain |
| `internal/payment`, `internal/feeds`, `internal/epochwatch` | the only packages that speak gRPC |
| `internal/postgres` | the only package that touches the database |
| `internal/wiring`, `cmd/` | the composition roots, the only code that uses dig |
| `contracts/` | the Solidity contracts and their Foundry tests |

## Data

PostgreSQL, through pgx and code that sqlc generates. Epochs and their accounts, every anchor attempt,
holders' registrations, the three logs' cursors, evidence batches with their leaves, and copies of the
committed fields of each lifecycle, delivery and review record. A second-generation epoch also keeps its
tree's root, the commitment to its total, its proof, and
[the blinding](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/migrations/00006_confidential_epochs.sql#L2-L12)
that lets the notary open that commitment to an auditor. A
[single-row lease](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/migrations/00001_notary.sql#L2-L7)
lets one writer anchor at a time. notary-watch keeps no database.

## Operating it

- Both long-running processes serve `/health/live`, `/health/ready` and `/metrics`.
- notaryd refuses to start against a chain or a contract it was not configured for. Before it serves
  anything it
  [checks the ledger, the council, the courts, the directory, the paymaster and the registry](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/cmd/notaryd/main.go#L110-L142),
  starting with
  [the chain id and the ledger's code](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/ethereum/ledger.go#L61-L93).
  It also
  [refuses an SRS file that is not the pinned one](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/wiring/scheme_two.go#L33-L35):
  the KZG parameters from the Aztec Ignition ceremony, built into the image and
  [held to a SHA-256](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/internal/plonk/production.go#L3-L9).
- The chain is public, so anyone can watch notary work: every proof and every anchor is a transaction to
  the addresses above.
