## Context

Kinetix holds other people's money — buyers' funds in escrow, merchants' and couriers' balances,
customers' wallets — and
[every one of those numbers was visible only inside payment](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L10-L16).
A holder could only take Kinetix's word that their balance was counted. The record of what happened to an
order, a delivery or a review lived in its owner's database, where its owner could change it.

## Decision

The notary's decisions were taken on
[2026-10-11](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L30),
and those that followed are dated in the same table.

- **Commit on a public chain.** Anyone can check a proof against the chain without trusting Kinetix, and
  nobody, Kinetix included, can rewrite an anchored commitment
  ([the purpose](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L10-L16)).
  Whatever a stranger must be able to check or enforce
  [lives in a contract](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L44).
- **A record, never a payment rail.** Indonesian law does not allow crypto as a means of payment, so the
  notary
  [never moves money on chain](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L25-L28).
- **A testnet, permanently: Base Sepolia**, with production-grade code, so that
  [moving to another network is an RPC endpoint, a chain id, keys and an address](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L36).
  The money is sandbox money, as payment's provider is
  [in sandbox in production too](/services/payment/#operating-it).
- **Immutable contracts**, replaced by
  [redeploying with a hash-chained history that a successor continues](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L37),
  and [a directory that says which generation counts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L56-L57).
- **No single key anchors.** A council of two, the notary and its watch, is the ledger's only anchorer,
  [with no spare Kinetix key](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L52):
  a third key would let Kinetix anchor without the watch. The contract
  [refuses a threshold below two](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/contracts/src/council/AnchorCouncil.sol#L21).
- **A lie and a silence are both provable on chain.** Courts let anyone prove that a signed statement is
  false, and let a holder prove they asked and got no answer, so
  [a holder left out can show it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L97-L99).
- **A holder needs no ether.** A paymaster pays for
  [a holder's own court calls, and only those](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L61).
- **Commitments, not content.** Each record is committed to with its own salt, so
  [nothing on chain names a buyer, an order or a review's text](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L1319-L1324),
  one log's outage stalls only its own chain of batches, and each log is
  [anchored daily, and only when it has new records](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L76).

## In effect

The contracts and their addresses are on the [notary](/services/notary/#on-chain) page. The council holds
[a threshold of two and is the ledger's only anchorer](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/deploy/base-sepolia.env#L29-L39).
How to check a proof is on the Verify pages:
[What a proof of liabilities proves](/verify/what-the-proof-proves/) and
[What the evidence proves](/verify/what-the-evidence-proves/).

## Consequences

- **A proof waits for finality**, which follows the layer below:
  [roughly fifteen to twenty minutes](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L204-L205),
  during which the verifier says the epoch is not final yet.
- **Testnets are retired.** The ledger can be sealed and continued on another network, and
  [the notary keeps every anchored header and receipt itself](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L183-L187),
  so history survives a network that does not.
- **A fix is a new generation**, never an upgrade in place.
- **The total is public** in the first commitment scheme, which is acceptable only because the money is
  sandbox money.
- **Evidence is provable the day after.** A batch closes once a day per log.
- **Both council members are run by Kinetix today.** The council stops a single stolen key from anchoring;
  it does not yet stop Kinetix itself, and
  [the watch is meant to move to a different operator](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L892-L897).

## Alternatives rejected

- **A payment rail on chain**: not allowed as a means of payment.
- **Upgradeable contracts**: turned down for immutable contracts, versioned by redeploying.
- **A single anchoring key, or a third Kinetix key in the council**: either would let Kinetix anchor alone.
- **Anchoring evidence hourly**: daily was chosen instead, for a proof the day after the event at a
  twenty-fourth of the gas.
- **A second verifier for the browser**: the page runs
  [the same Go verifier compiled to WebAssembly](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-notary-service/blob/0a5e21f2f06e9aa404692c437dc417bdded90215/PLAN.md#L65),
  so the page and the command line cannot disagree.
