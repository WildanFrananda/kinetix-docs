## Context

Kinetix needed full-text search over products, merchants and orders, in Indonesian and English.
Elasticsearch had been kept in the estate for that purpose, and the brief was
[an engine lighter than it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L17). Between the two real candidates, Meilisearch and Typesense,
[the licence decided](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L98-L109). Each licence was read from the project's own `LICENSE` on
the day.

## Decision

- The engine is **Typesense**, GPL-3.0 across the whole engine. It runs as a separate process reached over
  HTTP, so it is [neither linked into Kinetix code nor redistributed by it](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L102-L105).
- The client is `typesense-go/v3`, because [the v4 line is an alpha](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L121-L124).
- [Elasticsearch left the estate in the same change](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L27-L31).

## Consequences

- One licence covers the whole engine, so
  [no upgrade reopens the question of whether a feature is an enterprise one](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L111-L113).
- [Indonesian and English share one collection](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L126-L129). Typo tolerance does most of what
  a stemmer per language would, and synonyms are an explicit list.
- The memory Typesense needs here was
  [left to be measured with the real corpus](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L131-L134), not taken from published figures.
- The service runs in production: see [search](/services/search/).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Meilisearch.** It was plain MIT once and is now [`MIT AND BUSL-1.1`](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L102-L109): anything
  under its enterprise editions needs a commercial agreement for production use. See
  [ADR 0004](/decisions/0004-licence-changes-are-decided/).
- **Elasticsearch.** It is heavier, [981 MB as measured in this estate](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-search-service/blob/1316183858b32d1123bc5aa5c7502b74d3e5ceee/PLAN.md#L131-L132), and it
  would have needed an analyzer chain chosen per language.
