Goods reach the warehouse on a merchant's behalf, and staff book them onto a shelf. The merchant sells the product in
catalog, which catalog allows only once an admin has approved the seller
([Seller applications](/apis/rest/backoffice/seller-applications/)). Signed in as on
[Sign in](/apis/rest/backoffice/sign-in/), an admin finds the owner of each SKU in catalog, creates the bin the goods
go into, books the delivery, and later adjusts the count for damage, loss or miscounts. The three warehouse endpoints
need the admin's access token, which warehouse checks itself.

## 1. Find the owner of the goods

Read the product by the `sku` on the delivery and keep its `merchant_principal_id`, the identity principal of the
merchant who sells it, which steps 3 and 4 send. It must be that merchant: warehouse keeps stock per merchant and SKU
([bin_stock_service_handler.rb](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/a0eafe367f060d4f99aec06a07017d86b8bd046f/app/rpc/bin_stock_service_handler.rb#L32-L44)),
and catalog asks it for the stock of the product's own merchant
([product_service.py](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/95cabdb917607026a290b7d24e91b7121a0631d3/core/application/services/product_service.py#L92-L98)).
A `404` means no product with this SKU is on sale.

<RestEndpoint id="catalog.getProduct" />

## 2. Create the bin

Goods are booked into a bin by its `bin_code`, so the bin must exist first. A bin is created once, not per delivery:
the same request with a `bin_code` that exists updates that bin's zone and shelf level instead.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.upsertBin" />

## 3. Book the delivery

Send the `bin_code` from step 2, the `sku`, the units counted as `quantity`, the `merchant_principal_id` from step 1,
and an `idempotency_key` the client makes up for this one delivery and keeps until it has a final answer. The key goes
in the body; this endpoint reads no `Idempotency-Key` header. After a timeout, a `500` or a `503`, send the same body
with the same key: a delivery already booked answers `200` with `replayed: true` and is not booked twice.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.receiveStock" />

## 4. Adjust the stock

When goods are later found damaged, missing, miscounted, sent back to the supplier or expired, send the change as
`quantity_delta` with the matching `reason`, for the `bin_code`, `sku` and `merchant_principal_id` the stock was booked
under. Each adjustment takes a new `idempotency_key`, retried as in step 3. A removal that would cut into units
reserved for orders answers `409` with `max_reduction`, the largest removal possible now; a smaller removal is a new
adjustment with a new key.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.adjustStock" />
