Goods reach the warehouse on a merchant's behalf, and staff book them onto a shelf. The merchant sells the product in
catalog, which catalog allows only once an admin has approved the seller
([Seller applications](/apis/rest/backoffice/seller-applications/)). Signed in from Kinetix's staff network as on
[Sign in](/apis/rest/backoffice/sign-in/), an admin finds the owner of each SKU in catalog, chooses or creates the bin
the goods go into, books the delivery, reads what is on the shelves, adjusts the count for damage, loss or miscounts,
and reads back what was booked. Warehouse checks the admin's token itself on each of its endpoints. It records each
write with the admin's account and its outcome; a read is not recorded.

Warehouse reads every field strictly: a text field must be a JSON string, and a number a whole number within its
range, sent as a JSON integer or as a string written in digits; nothing is rounded or cut short. A value of the wrong
type, out of range or too long is answered `422` and changes nothing. The reads take their parameters in the query and
refuse one they cannot read with `400` and `INVALID_<PARAMETER>`
([api_ledger_query.rb](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/07fc2707413de029830bd1364e722a41b8285d81/app/controllers/concerns/api_ledger_query.rb#L49-L94)). They page by
keyset, never by offset: send the previous answer's cursor back, and stop when it is `null`.

## 1. Find the owner of the goods

Read the product by the `sku` on the delivery and keep its `merchant_principal_id`, the identity principal of the
merchant who sells it, which steps 3 and 5 send. It must be that merchant: warehouse keeps stock per merchant and SKU
([bin_stock_service_handler.rb](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/07fc2707413de029830bd1364e722a41b8285d81/app/rpc/bin_stock_service_handler.rb#L32-L46)),
and catalog asks it for the stock of the product's own merchant
([product_service.py](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-catalog-service/blob/ab557c90cb3230221c5a504def11fa7df26a6f7a/core/application/services/product_service.py#L113-L119)).
A `404` means no product with this SKU is on sale.

## 2. Choose or create the bin

Goods are booked into a bin by its `bin_code`, so the bin must exist first. List the bins to see their codes, zones and
shelf levels, and how many SKUs and units each holds; page with `after` set to `next_after` while it is not `null`.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.listBins" via="backoffice" />

A bin is created once, not per delivery: the same request with a `bin_code` that exists updates that bin's zone and
shelf level instead. A bin holds each SKU for one merchant only; a delivery of a SKU that the bin already holds for
another merchant is refused with `409`
([receive_stock_service.rb](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/07fc2707413de029830bd1364e722a41b8285d81/app/services/inventory/receive_stock_service.rb#L110-L113)), so read the bin's rows first
(step 4, with `bin_code`) when it is not empty.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.upsertBin" via="backoffice" />

## 3. Book the delivery

Send the `bin_code` from step 2, the `sku`, the units counted as `quantity`, the `merchant_principal_id` from step 1,
and an `idempotency_key` the client makes up for this one delivery and keeps until it has a final answer. The key goes
in the body; this endpoint reads no `Idempotency-Key` header. After a timeout, a `500` or a `503`, send the same body
with the same key, once the seconds in `Retry-After` have passed when the answer gives them, as warehouse's
`DATABASE_UNAVAILABLE` does: a delivery already booked answers `200` with `replayed: true` and is not booked twice.
A `400` or a `422` booked nothing and left the key unused, so the corrected body can go out with it
([receive_stock_service.rb](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/07fc2707413de029830bd1364e722a41b8285d81/app/services/inventory/receive_stock_service.rb#L64-L132)).
`422` `STOCK_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` means the bin cannot count that many units of the SKU: book the rest into another bin.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.receiveStock" via="backoffice" />

## 4. Read what is on the shelves

The stock is one row per bin and SKU, kept at zero once its units are gone. Narrow it with `sku` to find every bin that
holds a product, with `bin_code` to see one bin, or with `merchant_principal_id` for one merchant's goods; the filters
combine. Each row says what is on the shelf (`quantity`), what is held for orders (`reserved_quantity`) and what is
free (`available_quantity`), which is the most an adjustment in step 5 can remove
([adjust_stock_service.rb](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-warehouse-service/blob/07fc2707413de029830bd1364e722a41b8285d81/app/services/inventory/adjust_stock_service.rb#L106-L109)).
Page with `after` set to `next_after_id` while it is not `null`.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.listStock" via="backoffice" />

## 5. Adjust the stock

When goods are later found damaged, missing, miscounted, sent back to the supplier or expired, send the change as
`quantity_delta` with the matching `reason`, for the `bin_code`, `sku` and `merchant_principal_id` the stock was booked
under, as a row in step 4 shows them. Each adjustment takes a new `idempotency_key`, retried as in step 3. A removal that would cut into units
reserved for orders answers `409` with `max_reduction`, the largest removal possible now; a smaller removal is a new
adjustment with a new key.

<RestEndpoint id="warehouse.adjustStock" via="backoffice" />

## 6. Read the ledger

Every receipt and adjustment stays on record, newest first, with the same three filters as step 4: a receipt says who
booked how many units of which SKU into which bin for which merchant, an adjustment also the count before and after it
and its `reason`. A delivery sent again with the same key was booked once, so it appears once. Page with `before` set
to `next_before_id` while it is not `null`.
