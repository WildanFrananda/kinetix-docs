Matching tracks the couriers whose apps connect to it and gives each job, on its own, to an available courier near the
pickup. The backoffice watches the fleet and the dispatch board, and an admin steps in by hand: gives a waiting job to a
chosen courier, takes a job off its courier, or cancels a job no order is waiting for. Couriers join the fleet through
[Courier applications](/apis/rest/backoffice/courier-applications/); signed in from Kinetix's staff network as on
[Sign in](/apis/rest/backoffice/sign-in/), the admin reads the fleet, the board's counts, the jobs and one job, finds
couriers near a pickup, assigns, unassigns or cancels, and lists one courier's jobs. Kong checks the token on every
endpoint here, matching checks the role, and matching records every request on these routes with the admin's account and
its outcome
([router.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/router.ex#L24-L63)).

The courier's app holds a socket to matching on the topic `driver:<driver_id>`, and the channel subscribes to that
courier's dispatch events when it joins
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L49-L56)).
That is how steps 7, 8 and 9 reach the courier's app.

## 1. List the fleet

The couriers matching tracks right now, with each one's `status` and last position. Each `id` is a driver id, the
number steps 2, 7 and 10 take; jobs name their courier by the same number in `driver_id`.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.listDrivers" via="backoffice" />

## 2. Read one courier

Read one courier by the `id` from step 1, or by a job's `driver_id`.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.getDriver" via="backoffice" />

## 3. Read the board's counts

The counts for the head of the dispatch board: jobs waiting for a courier, jobs not yet finished, and jobs delivered
today.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.dispatchSummary" via="backoffice" />

## 4. List the jobs

Jobs come newest first, filtered by `status` when one is given; `status=pending` lists the jobs no courier holds. For
the next page, send the answer's `next_cursor` as `before`, until it is `null`.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.listDispatches" via="backoffice" />

## 5. Read one job

Read a job by its `id` from step 4. Keep its `pickup` for step 6; a delivered job also carries its proof of delivery.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.getDispatch" via="backoffice" />

## 6. Find couriers near the pickup

Send the job's `pickup.latitude` and `pickup.longitude` from step 5 as `latitude` and `longitude`, as plain numbers.
Matching reads each value whole and within its range: one with a unit or other text after it, a list, or a latitude
or longitude off the globe answers `400` `invalid_nearby_query`, and nothing was read. The answer lists tracked
couriers nearest first, whatever their duty status; only one whose `status` is `online` can take a job in step 7.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.nearbyDrivers" via="backoffice" />

## 7. Give a waiting job to a courier

Send the chosen courier's `id` from step 6 as `driver_id`, with the job's `id` in the path. On success matching pushes
`order_assigned` to that courier's socket
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L594-L600),
[driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L144-L147)),
carrying the job in this shape
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L237-L247)):

```json
{
  "id": 4813,
  "status": "assigned",
  "weight_kg": 2,
  "pickup": { "latitude": -6.1754, "longitude": 106.8272 },
  "dropoff": { "latitude": -6.2297, "longitude": 106.8295 },
  "assigned_at": "2026-10-22T03:15:02.187430Z"
}
```

The admin's choice overrides two checks automatic dispatch makes: matching compares neither the courier's capacity
with the job's `weight_kg` nor the age of the courier's last position
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L256-L269),
against [dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L529-L537)).
After a `500` or a `503`, from matching or from the edge, read the job again (step 5) before repeating: the
assignment may have been made, and one that failed while it was being written leaves the job `pending` and the courier
free
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L559-L592)).

<RestEndpoint id="matching.assignDispatch" via="backoffice" />

## 8. Take a job off its courier

Unassign a job whose `status` is `assigned`, with its `id` in the path. Matching pushes `order_unassigned` with the
job's `id` to the courier who held it, then `active_order`, that courier's open job or `null`
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L373-L386),
[driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L154-L158)).
The job is `pending` again, for matching to offer to another courier or for step 7.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.unassignDispatch" via="backoffice" />

## 9. Cancel a job

Only a job with no `order_number` can be cancelled: a job that carries one, as every job order books does, answers
`409` `bound_to_order` whatever its status. When the cancelled job was `assigned`, matching pushes `order_updated`
with the job, `status` `cancelled`, to the courier who held it
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L652-L663),
[dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L693-L698),
[driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L149-L152));
a `pending` job has no courier to tell.

<RestEndpoint id="matching.cancelDispatch" via="backoffice" />
