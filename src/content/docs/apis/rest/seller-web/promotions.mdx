A seller's promotions live in pricing: discounts on unit prices, vouchers that buyers type at checkout, and
flash sales of one product. Each is created, then read back; once created, a promotion cannot be changed or
ended early. Every create takes an access token whose role is `seller`, for a merchant identity lets trade, and
an `Idempotency-Key` header with a new key for each promotion. After a timeout, a `500` or a `503`, the client
sends the same body again with the same key: pricing records the key in the same transaction as the promotion,
so one key creates at most one promotion
([discount_repository.rs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/d3020a29ca7ac6fcc2d5a7e472a6b234cc783d0d/src/repositories/discount_repository.rs#L45-L98),
[promotion_creation_ledger.rs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/d3020a29ca7ac6fcc2d5a7e472a6b234cc783d0d/src/repositories/promotion_creation_ledger.rs#L21-L62)).
A browser may send `Idempotency-Key` and read `Idempotent-Replayed` ([Browsers](/apis/rest/#browsers)).

## 1. Create a discount

A discount takes a percentage or a fixed amount off the unit price between `start_time` and `end_time`. It
targets one of the seller's products by its SKU in `target_product_id`
([Products](/apis/rest/seller-web/products/), step 2), a category by its `slug` in `target_category_id`
(Products, step 1), or, with neither, every product of the seller's. Keep `id`.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.createDiscount" />

## 2. Read the discounts in force

The list holds every discount in force: the platform's and every merchant's. The seller's own carry its
`user.principalId` in `merchant_principal_id`
([identity_grpc.controller.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/4b8eb5e5f70f05a8a5f9b6bd0d04b2bf88cc8b7f/src/adapters/controllers/identity_grpc.controller.ts#L240-L242)).
A discount is listed only between its `start_time` and its `end_time`, so one that starts later is not listed
yet.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.listDiscounts" />

## 3. Create a voucher

The seller chooses the `code` buyers will type and the voucher's terms. The voucher applies only to carts of
the seller's goods. Keep `code` as the answer gives it, in upper case.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.createVoucher" />

## 4. Read a voucher

By the `code` from step 3, with the seller's access token, so that the answer includes `quota` and
`used_count`.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.getVoucher" />

## 5. Create a flash sale

A flash sale sells a limited number of units of one of the seller's products at `flash_price`. `product_id` is
the product's SKU (Products, step 2). Keep `id`.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.createFlashSale" />

## 6. Read the flash sale

By the product's SKU: the sale a buyer of that product gets now, and the units left at its price. A sale that
has not reached its `start_time` is not shown yet.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.getFlashSale" />
