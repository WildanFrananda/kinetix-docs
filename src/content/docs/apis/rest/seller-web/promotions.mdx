A seller's promotions live in pricing: discounts on unit prices, vouchers that buyers type at checkout, and
flash sales of one product. Each is created, then read back; once created, a promotion cannot be changed or
ended early. Every create needs a session whose role is `seller`, for a merchant identity lets trade, and an
`Idempotency-Key` header with a new key for each promotion, which the edge passes on, as it passes
`Idempotent-Replayed` back
([forwarding.tl](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-edge-service/blob/0a52d5805b57a818b16b05848b8333d34115f682/config/forwarding.tl#L11-L19)).
Pricing looks the key up before it checks the promotion's terms or asks identity and catalog, and records it in
the same transaction as the promotion, so one key creates at most one promotion
([discount_routes.rs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/0ed7db4cd6fb8d40375cab26dd7ac56ebce16a8a/src/routes/discount_routes.rs#L22-L51),
[promotion_creation_ledger.rs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-pricing-service/blob/0ed7db4cd6fb8d40375cab26dd7ac56ebce16a8a/src/repositories/promotion_creation_ledger.rs#L21-L82)).
After a timeout, a `500`, a `503` or the edge's `503` `API_UNAVAILABLE`, the client sends the same body again with
the same key: the answer is the promotion the first request created, marked `Idempotent-Replayed: true`, whatever
has changed since. A `422` `UNPROCESSABLE` means the key was used for another body, so a new promotion needs a new
key.

## 1. Create a discount

A discount takes a percentage or a fixed amount off the unit price between `start_time` and `end_time`. It
targets one of the seller's products by its SKU in `target_product_id`
([Products](/apis/rest/seller-web/products/), step 1), a category by its `slug` in `target_category_id`
(Products, step 2), or, with neither, every product of the seller's. Keep `id`.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.createDiscount" via="seller-web" />

## 2. Read the discounts in force

The list holds every discount in force: the platform's and every merchant's. The seller's own carry its
`principalId` ([Sign in](/apis/rest/seller-web/sign-in/), step 3) in `merchant_principal_id`
([identity_grpc.controller.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/14a5d4291f2e4987e2ef774f68dc3888c509617c/src/adapters/controllers/identity_grpc.controller.ts#L240-L242)).
A discount is listed only between its `start_time` and its `end_time`, so one that starts later is not listed
yet.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.listDiscounts" via="seller-web" />

## 3. Create a voucher

The seller chooses the `code` buyers will type and the voucher's terms. The voucher applies only to carts of
the seller's goods. Keep `code` as the answer gives it, in upper case.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.createVoucher" via="seller-web" />

## 4. Read a voucher

By the `code` from step 3. The edge sends the seller's access token with it, so the answer includes `quota` and
`used_count`.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.getVoucher" via="seller-web" />

## 5. Create a flash sale

A flash sale sells a limited number of units of one of the seller's products at `flash_price`. `product_id` is
the product's SKU (Products, step 1). Keep `id`.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.createFlashSale" via="seller-web" />

## 6. Read the flash sale

By the product's SKU: the sale a buyer of that product gets now, and the units left at its price. A sale that
has not reached its `start_time` is not shown yet.

<RestEndpoint id="pricing.getFlashSale" via="seller-web" />
