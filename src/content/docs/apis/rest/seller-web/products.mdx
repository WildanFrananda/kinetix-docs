A seller's products live in catalog. The seller lists its own products, picks a category, creates a product, reads
it as buyers see it, changes it and withdraws it. Every write asks identity whether the account's merchant may
trade, so the account must have been approved ([Become a seller](/apis/rest/seller-web/become-a-seller/)). The list
reads the role from the session's access token instead, so it answers once the session carries `seller`
(Become a seller, step 5). Stock is not set here; it arrives through the backoffice's
[stock intake](/apis/rest/backoffice/stock-intake/).

## 1. List the seller's products

The products screen starts here, and the list is how the application finds a product's `id` again, withdrawn
products included. Ask for the next `page` until the pages read hold `count` products. `id` addresses a product in
steps 5 and 6; `is_active` `false` marks one that is withdrawn.

<RestEndpoint id="catalog.listMyProducts" via="seller-web" />

## 2. Choose a category

Every product belongs to one category. Send the chosen category's `id` as `category_id` in step 3. Its `slug`
is what a category discount names ([Promotions](/apis/rest/seller-web/promotions/), step 1).

<RestEndpoint id="catalog.listCategories" via="seller-web" />

## 3. Create the product

The seller chooses the `sku` and enters the rest; `category_id` comes from step 2. The `201` carries the product's
`id`, and step 1 lists it from then on. The create takes no idempotency key: when its answer is lost, reading the
product by its SKU (step 4) shows whether it exists, whose it is (`merchant_principal_id`) and its `id`.

<RestEndpoint id="catalog.createProduct" via="seller-web" />

## 4. Read the product as buyers see it

By the `sku` from step 3. `warehouse_stock` reads `out_of_stock` until stock for the SKU has been received
through the backoffice's stock intake.

<RestEndpoint id="catalog.getProduct" via="seller-web" />

## 5. Change the product

Send only the fields that change, with the product's `id` from step 1 or step 3. `is_active` `false` withdraws
the product as step 6 does, and `true` puts a withdrawn product back on sale.

<RestEndpoint id="catalog.updateProduct" via="seller-web" />

## 6. Withdraw the product

With the same `id`. The product stays in catalog with its SKU and in step 1's list, and step 5 with `is_active`
`true` puts it back on sale.

<RestEndpoint id="catalog.deleteProduct" via="seller-web" />
