[Checkout](/apis/rest/buyer-app/checkout/) pays from the buyer's Kinetix wallet, which payment keeps, so a
buyer tops the wallet up first. The wallet endpoints accept only a `customer`. The app reads the balance
(step 1) and starts a top-up (step 2); the buyer pays outside Kinetix (step 3); Midtrans tells payment
(step 4); the app reads the top-up's outcome (step 5).

## 1. Read the balance

`availableBalance` is what checkout can debit. A wallet that money has never reached answers `exists: false`
at zero rather than an error.

<RestEndpoint id="payment.customerBalance" />

## 2. Start a top-up

The app makes a new `Idempotency-Key` for each top-up the buyer starts, stores it before sending, and sends
it again with the same body when a request gets no answer: payment answers from the top-up that key started,
if there is one, so nothing is charged twice. From the `202` the app keeps `referenceNumber` for step 5 and
shows `instructions` to the buyer. A `503` that carries `referenceNumber` names a top-up that stays `PENDING`
and may still be paid, so the app follows it in step 5 instead of starting another.

A top-up may not take the wallet above IDR 2,000,000, and every top-up still `PENDING` counts against that
as if it were paid, until it leaves `PENDING`
([TopUpService.java](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/ae1f3e07d4849f51bbc41b8cb5dd356dcfa5d793/modules/application/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/application/TopUpService.java#L446-L459)).
A top-up cannot be cancelled. So the app keeps the `referenceNumber` of each top-up until it leaves `PENDING`,
and on a `422 WALLET_BALANCE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` while one is pending, it takes the buyer back to that top-up's
`instructions` (step 5) rather than offering a new one.

<RestEndpoint id="payment.customerTopUp" />

## 3. The buyer pays

This happens outside the API. The buyer transfers the amount from a bank to the virtual account in
`instructions.virtualAccountNumber`, or scans the QR code at `instructions.qrCodeUrl` with a payment app
that takes QRIS, before `instructions.expiresAt`. Midtrans issued these details when payment created the
charge, with the top-up's `referenceNumber` as Midtrans' `order_id`
([MidtransPaymentGatewayAdapter.java](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/ae1f3e07d4849f51bbc41b8cb5dd356dcfa5d793/modules/infrastructure/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/infrastructure/gateway/MidtransPaymentGatewayAdapter.java#L190-L224),
[instructions](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/ae1f3e07d4849f51bbc41b8cb5dd356dcfa5d793/modules/infrastructure/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/infrastructure/gateway/MidtransPaymentGatewayAdapter.java#L325-L349)).

## 4. Midtrans notifies payment

Midtrans sends this request to payment; the app never calls it. Its `order_id` is the top-up's
`referenceNumber`. payment checks the signature, asks Midtrans for the transaction's status and credits the
wallet once it has settled
([MidtransNotificationController.java](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-payment-service/blob/ae1f3e07d4849f51bbc41b8cb5dd356dcfa5d793/modules/api/src/main/java/com/kinetix/payment/api/controller/MidtransNotificationController.java#L30-L55)).
It is listed here because it is what moves the top-up out of `PENDING`.

<RestEndpoint id="payment.midtransNotification" />

## 5. Read the top-up's outcome

`{referenceNumber}` is the one from step 2. This reads payment's own record, so the app reads it again until
`status` is no longer `PENDING`; while it is, the answer carries the `instructions` the buyer pays by. On
`SUCCESS` the amount is in the wallet, and the app reads the balance again as in step 1.

<RestEndpoint id="payment.customerTopUpStatus" />
