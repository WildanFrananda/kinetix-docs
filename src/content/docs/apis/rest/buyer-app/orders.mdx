order answers the orders whose buyer is the access token's `sub`. A buyer app lists them (step 1) and opens
one (step 2). The order that [checkout](/apis/rest/buyer-app/checkout/) placed is read the same way.

## 1. List the orders

Newest first, and orders placed at the same instant by `id`, so every order has one place in the list
([OrderService.cs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-order-service/blob/1395062e2b803ead17d09bc8f50622367c7fd2ab/Application/Services/OrderService.cs#L637-L642)).
`status` narrows the list to one status. The app asks for the next `page` while `page` × `pageSize` is below
`totalCount`, up to `page` 10,000, the last one order serves (`maxPage`). Pages are cut by position, so an
order placed while the app pages makes the next page repeat orders already shown: the app drops repeats by
`id`, and reads page 1 again to show orders placed since. Each item's `id` opens the order in step 2.

<RestEndpoint id="order.listMyOrders" />

## 2. Read one order

`{orderId}` is an item's `id` from step 1, or `id` from checkout's answer; the `orderNumber` is not accepted
here. The answer shows the order's `status`, its lines and its amounts, and `driverPrincipalId` once a
courier has delivered it. A delivered order can be [reviewed](/apis/rest/buyer-app/reviews/), and its
history [proven](/apis/rest/buyer-app/proofs/#5-fetch-an-orders-evidence).
