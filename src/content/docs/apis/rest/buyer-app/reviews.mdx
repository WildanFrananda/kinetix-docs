Once an order is delivered, its buyer reviews each product in it and rates the courier who delivered it.
review reads the order from order itself, so a request names only the order and the product; the merchant
and the courier come from the order
([GrpcOrderClient.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/531cdb5506d80532bf11bcbb1e84c5bab5115709/app/Clients/GrpcOrderClient.php#L144-L158)).
A buyer app reads the order (step 1), reviews its products (step 2) and rates the courier (step 3). Steps 2
and 3 do not depend on each other.

## 1. Read the order

The app offers reviews only when `status` is `DELIVERED` or `COMPLETED`, the two statuses review accepts
([GrpcOrderClient.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/531cdb5506d80532bf11bcbb1e84c5bab5115709/app/Clients/GrpcOrderClient.php#L32-L35),
[ProductReviewService.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/531cdb5506d80532bf11bcbb1e84c5bab5115709/app/Services/ProductReviewService.php#L33-L35)).
It keeps the order's `id`, each line's `productId`, and `driverPrincipalId`.

<RestEndpoint id="order.getOrder" />

## 2. Review each product

One request per line of the order: `order_id` is the order's `id` (not its `orderNumber`), and `product_id`
the line's `productId`. The app keeps the review's id, which its
[proof](/apis/rest/buyer-app/proofs/#6-fetch-a-reviews-evidence) takes: `id` from a `201`, or `review_id` from a
`409`, which means the line already has the buyer's review and the app shows it as done. A write whose outcome
is unknown, after a `503` or no answer, is sent again unchanged, and ends in one of these two answers.

<RestEndpoint id="review.createProductReview" />

## 3. Rate the courier

One request per order, and only when step 1 showed a `driverPrincipalId`: `order_id` is the order's `id`. As in
step 2, the app keeps the rating's id, `id` from a `201` or `review_id` from a `409` that says the rating is
already done, and sends a write of unknown outcome again unchanged.
