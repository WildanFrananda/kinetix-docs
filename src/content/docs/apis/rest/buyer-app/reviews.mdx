Once an order is delivered, its buyer reviews each product in it and rates the courier who delivered it.
review reads the order from order itself, so a request names only the order and the product; the merchant
and the courier come from the order
([GrpcOrderClient.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/e3ca897c68947dd7ba4a47f04355e5e91da9afa8/app/Clients/GrpcOrderClient.php#L144-L158)).
A buyer app reads the order (step 1), reviews its products (step 2) and rates the courier (step 3). Steps 2
and 3 do not depend on each other.

## 1. Read the order

The app offers reviews only when `status` is `DELIVERED` or `COMPLETED`, the two statuses review accepts
([GrpcOrderClient.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/e3ca897c68947dd7ba4a47f04355e5e91da9afa8/app/Clients/GrpcOrderClient.php#L32-L35),
[ProductReviewService.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/e3ca897c68947dd7ba4a47f04355e5e91da9afa8/app/Services/ProductReviewService.php#L34-L36)).
It keeps the order's `id`, each line's `productId`, and `driverPrincipalId`.

<RestEndpoint id="order.getOrder" />

## 2. Review each product

One request per line of the order: `order_id` is the order's `id` as order answered it, and `product_id` the
line's `productId`. review takes `order_id` only as a UUID, and refuses the `orderNumber` with a `422` before
it asks order; the app also takes NUL characters (U+0000) out of the buyer's `comment`, which review refuses
the same way
([ProductReviewController.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/e3ca897c68947dd7ba4a47f04355e5e91da9afa8/app/Http/Controllers/ProductReviewController.php#L23-L28)).
review keeps the order's id in lower case, so its letter case does not matter
([ProductReviewService.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/e3ca897c68947dd7ba4a47f04355e5e91da9afa8/app/Services/ProductReviewService.php#L19-L24)).
The app keeps the review's id, which its [proof](/apis/rest/buyer-app/proofs/#6-fetch-a-reviews-evidence)
takes: `id` from a `201`, or `review_id` from a `409`, which means the line already has the buyer's review and
the app shows it as done. A write whose outcome is unknown, after a `503` or no answer, is sent again
unchanged, and ends in one of these two answers.

<RestEndpoint id="review.createProductReview" />

## 3. Rate the courier

One request per order, and only when step 1 showed a `driverPrincipalId`: `order_id` is the order's `id`.
`order_id` and `comment` follow the same rules as in step 2
([DriverRatingController.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/e3ca897c68947dd7ba4a47f04355e5e91da9afa8/app/Http/Controllers/DriverRatingController.php#L23-L27)).
As in step 2, the app keeps the rating's id, `id` from a `201` or `review_id` from a `409` that says the rating
is already done, and sends a write of unknown outcome again unchanged.
