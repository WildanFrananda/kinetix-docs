Once an order is delivered, its buyer reviews each product in it and rates the courier who delivered it.
review reads the order from order itself, so a request names only the order and the product; the merchant
and the courier come from the order
([GrpcOrderClient.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/173ec20a938bc56ec4df193f5e763dae9901d0a9/app/Clients/GrpcOrderClient.php#L144-L158)).
A buyer app reads the order (step 1), reviews its products (step 2) and rates the courier (step 3). Steps 2
and 3 do not depend on each other.

## 1. Read the order

The app offers reviews only when `status` is `DELIVERED` or `COMPLETED`, the two statuses review accepts
([GrpcOrderClient.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/173ec20a938bc56ec4df193f5e763dae9901d0a9/app/Clients/GrpcOrderClient.php#L32-L35),
[ProductReviewService.php](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-review-service/blob/173ec20a938bc56ec4df193f5e763dae9901d0a9/app/Services/ProductReviewService.php#L33-L35)).
It keeps the order's `id`, each line's `productId`, and `driverPrincipalId`.

<RestEndpoint id="order.getOrder" />

## 2. Review each product

One request per line of the order: `order_id` is the order's `id` (not its `orderNumber`), and `product_id`
the line's `productId`. A `409` means the line already has a review, so the app shows it as done. From the
`201` the app keeps the review's `id`, which its [proof](/apis/rest/buyer-app/proofs/#6-fetch-a-reviews-evidence)
takes.

<RestEndpoint id="review.createProductReview" />

## 3. Rate the courier

One request per order, and only when step 1 showed a `driverPrincipalId`: `order_id` is the order's `id`. A
`409` means the rating is already done. The app keeps the rating's `id` from the `201`, as in step 2.

<RestEndpoint id="review.createDriverRating" />
