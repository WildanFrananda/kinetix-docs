Two reads a courier client makes about the courier's own account: the driver wallet in payment, where delivery
fees are held and then released to the courier, and the ratings buyers gave the courier in review. Neither
depends on the other, and both follow [signing in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/). The courier app at commit
`86dd282` calls neither: its only HTTP requests to Kinetix are the identity and matching calls of its API client
([driver_api.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/auth/driver_api.dart#L8-L36))
and the photo upload of [Go on duty and deliver](/apis/rest/courier-app/deliver/).

## 1. Read the driver wallet

Send an access token whose role is `courier`. A token issued before the admin approved the courier still says
`customer` and gets `403`; [refresh](/apis/rest/courier-app/register/) once after approval. The wallet is always
the caller's own, so the path names no one.

<RestEndpoint id="payment.driverBalance" />

## 2. List the courier's ratings

The path takes the courier's principal id: `user.principalId` in the answer to signing in, to registration or
to a refresh. The listing is public and needs no token. Ask for the next page with `page` while it is below
`last_page`.
