Most of a courier's work goes over the unified `camelCase` JSON event WebSocket handled by edge and relayed
upstream to matching, rather than HTTP. The courier opens the socket, immediately receives `matching.job.snapshot`,
goes on duty and sends positions, and receives the job matching assigns; there is no offer to accept or decline.
Carrying the job, the courier reports the pickup, asks matching for an upload address, puts the proof photo straight
into storage over HTTP, and reports the delivery with the photo and the receiver's signature. Before any of this the
courier has [signed in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/): the access token must carry the role `courier` and be
current when the socket opens ([Stay signed in](/apis/rest/courier-app/stay-signed-in/)).

## 1. Open the driver socket

A WebSocket connection to `wss://api.wildanfrananda.my.id/ws/v1/events` with `Authorization: Bearer <accessToken>`
([event_socket.tl](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-edge-service/blob/8c0fa9447c9d9fea964540ed1f8d032a3341b702/src/http/event_socket.tl#L601-L630)).
Edge verifies the Bearer token with identity's `/api/v1/auth/me`, then opens matching's internal upstream
WebSocket (`WebSock`) with the same token and `X-Edge-Role: courier`
([#L331-L360](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-edge-service/blob/8c0fa9447c9d9fea964540ed1f8d032a3341b702/src/http/event_socket.tl#L331-L360),
[event_socket_plug.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching_web/event_socket_plug.ex#L24-L48)).
Kong counts each connection against the route's [limit per address](/apis/rest/#limits).

<RestSocket id="matching.driverSocket" />

When `401` or `403` is returned during upgrade, the client refreshes the session and connects again
([connection_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/connection_repository_impl.dart#L95-L134)),
and asks `GET /api/v1/matching/driver/me` to distinguish `pending_approval` from missing driver records
([#L136-L177](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/connection_repository_impl.dart#L136-L177)).
After a transient answer or lost connection, the courier app backs off 1, 2, 4 and up to 30 seconds
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L409-L429)).

## 2. Receive the initial snapshot

Every frame is a `camelCase` JSON object with `event`, `schemaVersion: 1`, optional `requestId` for command-reply
correlation, and `data`
([envelope.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching_web/events/envelope.ex#L24-L70)).
There is no channel join step: right after upgrade, matching pushes `matching.job.snapshot` with `dutyStatus` and the
courier's `activeOrder`, so a courier who reconnects mid-delivery resumes at once; step 7 says how the app treats it:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.snapshot" />

Frames without a valid JSON `event` or with a `schemaVersion` other than `1` receive `command.error`
(`INVALID_PAYLOAD` or `UNSUPPORTED_SCHEMA_VERSION`), and unknown command names receive `UNKNOWN_EVENT`
([event_socket.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching_web/event_socket.ex#L203-L234),
[#L380-L382](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching_web/event_socket.ex#L380-L382)).

## 3. Keep the socket alive

Both edge and matching enforce a 60-second WebSocket idle timeout
([event_socket.tl](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-edge-service/blob/8c0fa9447c9d9fea964540ed1f8d032a3341b702/src/http/event_socket.tl#L28),
[event_socket_plug.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching_web/event_socket_plug.ex#L45-L48)),
so a courier who sends no position keeps the socket open with `system.ping`:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.heartbeat" />

The courier app sends a heartbeat every 30 seconds (and early when a command goes unanswered for 5 seconds), closing
and reconnecting if a heartbeat goes unanswered for 10 seconds
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L32-L35),
[#L362-L407](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L362-L407)),
then sets `online` duty again as step 4 describes.

## 4. Go on duty

Send `courier.duty.set` with `online`. When the socket terminates, matching sets the courier `offline`
([event_socket.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching_web/event_socket.ex#L193-L198),
[driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching/tracking/driver_state.ex#L258-L261),
[#L290-L293](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching/tracking/driver_state.ex#L290-L293)),
so a courier on duty sends it again after every reconnect:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.status" />

The courier app asks for location permission and starts GPS before setting duty online
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L76-L111)).

## 5. Send the live position

Automatic dispatch considers only couriers whose position arrived after they went on duty
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching/tracking/driver_state.ex#L137-L145),
[dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching/dispatch.ex#L532-L540)),
so `courier.location.ping` follows step 4 at once:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.ping" />

When the courier holds an active `assigned` or `picked_up` job bound to a storefront `orderNumber`, matching also
publishes each accepted ping as `courier.location.updated` onto Redis Pub/Sub `matching:position:{orderNumber}` for
subscribed buyers
([event_socket.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching_web/event_socket.ex#L385-L401)).
The courier app sends a ping right away, then every 4 seconds when it has moved at least 5 metres, falling back to
step 6 offline buffering when disconnected or `RATE_LIMITED`
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L208-L218),
[#L254-L289](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L254-L289)).

## 6. Send positions recorded offline

Positions the phone recorded while offline go in `courier.location.batch` once the socket is back, never as live
`courier.location.ping` frames:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.pingBatch" />

The courier app keeps offline coordinates per signed-in courier, drops readings older than 24 hours, flushes batches
oldest first, and deletes a batch only once `command.ack` arrives (`refused` entries included)
([offline_position_store.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/offline/offline_position_store.dart#L48-L86),
[duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L380-L476)).

## 7. Receive the job

There is no offer to accept or decline. matching assigns a job, automatically to the nearest `online` courier
whose vehicle can carry it, or by an admin's choice, and pushes `matching.job.assigned` over the socket
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching/dispatch.ex#L524-L540),
[#L600-L604](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching/dispatch.ex#L600-L604)).
Beside `matching.job.snapshot` from step 2, three server pushes concern the courier's job:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.orderAssigned" />

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.orderUpdated" />

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.orderUnassigned" />

`data.order.id` is what every later step sends as `orderId`. A job's screen closes as cancelled on
`matching.job.updated` with `cancelled`, as unassigned on `matching.job.unassigned` (followed by `matching.job.snapshot`),
or as inactive on `matching.job.snapshot` with `activeOrder: null`
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L213-L262),
[order_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/order_view_model.dart#L185-L211)).

## 8. Pick up the parcel

At the merchant, send `matching.job.pickup` with the job's `id` as `orderId`:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.pickup" />

Because a repeated pickup changes nothing, the client can safely retry until `command.ack` arrives
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L39-L44),
[retry_mixin.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/mixins/retry_mixin.dart#L4-L45)).

## 9. Ask for the photo's upload address

Hash the JPEG photo's exact bytes with SHA-256 and send `matching.job.podUpload` with `orderId` and `photoSha256`:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.podUpload" />

Keep `uploadUrl` and `uploadHeaders` for step 10 and `photoUrl` for step 11. The courier app verifies the JPEG
magic bytes (`FF D8 FF`) before hashing
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L46-L78)).

## 10. Upload the photo to storage

An HTTP `PUT` of the JPEG's bytes to `uploadUrl`, unchanged, with `uploadHeaders` as returned and no token. Storage
checks the signature and SHA-256 digest (`2xx` and `412` alike mean the object is stored)
([pod_photo_uploader.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/storage/pod_photo_uploader.dart#L17-L38)).

<RestEndpoint id="matching.uploadProofPhoto" />

## 11. Report the delivery

Send `matching.job.delivered` with `orderId`, `photoUrl` from step 9 as `podPhotoUrl`, and the receiver's PNG
signature as `podSignature`:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.delivered" />

The courier app captures the signature on screen as PNG
([order_view.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/ui/widgets/order/order_view.dart#L102-L147)),
retries until answered and on `PROOF_UNVERIFIABLE`, and drafts proof locally for up to 24 hours
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L135-L181),
[proof_draft_store.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/storage/proof_draft_store.dart#L17-L112)).

## 12. Go off duty

At the end of the shift, send `courier.duty.set` with `offline`, the message of step 4. matching then offers the
courier no more jobs
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/c09bc12e39aa22a5d4bb1b17c65011323a897562/lib/kinetix_matching/tracking/driver_state.ex#L221-L223)).
[Signing out](/apis/rest/courier-app/stay-signed-in/) also closes the socket.
