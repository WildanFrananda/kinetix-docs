Most of a courier's work goes over matching's driver socket, a Phoenix channel, rather than HTTP. The courier
opens the socket, joins the channel `driver:<driver id>`, goes on duty and sends positions, and receives the
job matching assigns; there is no offer to accept or decline. Carrying the job, the courier reports the pickup,
asks matching for an upload address, puts the proof photo straight into storage over HTTP, and reports the
delivery with the photo and the receiver's signature. Before any of this the courier has
[signed in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/): the driver id is `data.id` from that page's last step, and the
access token must carry the role `courier` and be current when the socket opens
([Stay signed in](/apis/rest/courier-app/stay-signed-in/)).

## 1. Open the driver socket

A WebSocket connection to a fixed address. The client sets one header itself, `Sec-WebSocket-Protocol`, with the
access token in place of the placeholder; the WebSocket library adds the upgrade headers. Kong passes this path,
and the same path ending in `/websocket`, which Phoenix's JavaScript client dials, to matching without checking
the token, and counts each connection against the route's [limit per address](/apis/rest/#limits)
([gateway.pkl](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-api-gateway/blob/45fea5cd0e07d5bdbe372b63fb0bdac773f64c5d/config/gateway.pkl#L426-L439)).

<RestSocket id="matching.driverSocket" />

The courier app builds this address and these subprotocols
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L86-L97)),
and opens the socket only for a session that knows its driver id; a session kept while approval was pending has
none, and shows the waiting screen instead
([#L202-L216](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L202-L216)).
It counts only `401` and `403` as a refusal
([handshake_refused_exception.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/handshake_refused_exception.dart#L8-L10)):
then it refreshes the session and connects again
([connection_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/connection_repository_impl.dart#L95-L134)).
When the new token is refused too, before a connection has held, it asks `GET /api/v1/matching/driver/me` why:
on `pending_approval` it shows the waiting screen and keeps the session, on `401`, another `403` or `404` it ends
the session, and on a `200` naming another driver id it stores that id and connects again
([#L136-L177](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/connection_repository_impl.dart#L136-L177)).
After any other answer or a lost connection it connects again after 1, 2, 4 and up to 30 seconds, each wait
lengthened by up to one second at random
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L409-L429)).

## 2. Join the courier's channel

Every frame is a JSON array `[join_ref, ref, topic, event, payload]`; a reply repeats the `ref` of the frame it
answers, and a push from matching has `null` for `ref`
([v2_json_serializer.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/b5026660dc744424545e7d2e3b7547507443ab40/lib/phoenix/socket/serializers/v2_json_serializer.ex#L63-L98)).
Join the topic `driver:` followed by the driver id:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.join" />

Right after the join, matching pushes the courier's current job, so a courier who reconnects mid-delivery is
back on that job at once; step 7 says how the app treats it:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.activeOrder" />

The courier app sends every later frame with the join's `ref` as `join_ref` and a new `ref`
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L154-L180),
[#L272-L297](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L272-L297)).
It handles `forbidden` and `invalid_topic` as it handles a refused token in step 1, so a driver id that is no
longer the courier's is replaced by the one `GET /api/v1/matching/driver/me` names, and connects again after
any other refusal, `join crashed` included
([#L37-L44](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L37-L44),
[#L254-L264](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L254-L264)).

Phoenix itself sends two frames the blocks do not show. When the channel fails, for example because matching's
database did not answer, Phoenix pushes `phx_error` with the join's `ref` in both reference places, and the
channel is gone until it is joined again; a second `phx_join` for the same topic replaces the joined channel,
which Phoenix closes with `phx_close` first
([socket.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/b5026660dc744424545e7d2e3b7547507443ab40/lib/phoenix/socket.ex#L560-L569),
[#L769-L786](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/b5026660dc744424545e7d2e3b7547507443ab40/lib/phoenix/socket.ex#L769-L786),
[#L866-L898](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/b5026660dc744424545e7d2e3b7547507443ab40/lib/phoenix/socket.ex#L866-L898));
the courier app treats both as a lost connection
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L345-L349)).
The answers to frames on a topic that is not joined, and the silence for frames with an old `join_ref`, come from
the same file
([#L803-L842](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/b5026660dc744424545e7d2e3b7547507443ab40/lib/phoenix/socket.ex#L803-L842)).
An event the channel does not know is answered `unknown_event`, and the channel stays joined
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L133-L135)).

## 3. Keep the socket alive

The 60-second limit is Phoenix's idle timeout for a WebSocket
([websocket.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/b5026660dc744424545e7d2e3b7547507443ab40/lib/phoenix/transports/websocket.ex#L26-L35)),
so a courier who sends no position still sends a heartbeat:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.heartbeat" />

The courier app sends one every 30 seconds from the moment the join succeeds, and one at once when a push has had
no reply for 5 seconds. When a heartbeat has no reply within 10 seconds, it closes the socket and connects again
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L32-L35),
[#L172-L179](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L172-L179),
[#L362-L407](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L362-L407)),
then joins, and declares duty again as step 4 describes.

## 4. Go on duty

Push `status` with `online`. When the channel ends, because the socket was closed or dropped, matching sets the
courier `offline`
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L258-L261),
[#L290-L293](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L290-L293)),
so a courier on duty sends it again after every new join:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.status" />

The courier app does so; the first time, it asks for location permission and starts the GPS before pushing
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L76-L111),
[#L127-L143](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L127-L143)).

## 5. Send the live position

Automatic dispatch considers only couriers whose position arrived after they went on duty
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L137-L145),
[dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L529-L537)),
so the first `ping` follows step 4 at once:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.ping" />

The courier app sends one at once, then, every 4 seconds, the newest position when it has moved at least 5
metres
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L21-L22),
[#L208-L218](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L208-L218),
[#L254-L278](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L254-L278)).
A position it cannot send live is kept for step 6: while the socket is down, before `status` `online` has been
answered, when a `ping` gets no reply, and for the 10 seconds it pauses after `rate_limited`; when the pause
ends, the newest position goes live
([#L280-L289](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L280-L289),
[#L306-L352](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L306-L352),
[#L478-L490](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L478-L490)).

## 6. Send positions recorded offline

Positions the phone recorded while it had no connection go in `ping_batch` once the socket is back, never as
live `ping`s:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.pingBatch" />

The courier app keeps each position it could not send under the courier who was on duty, and builds a batch only
from that courier's positions, oldest first, from the same fields as a `ping`; positions another courier left on
the phone wait for that courier's next sign-in, and positions older than 24 hours are dropped before a batch is
built
([offline_position_store.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/offline/offline_position_store.dart#L48-L86),
[channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L119-L131),
[#L182-L188](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L182-L188),
[duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L380-L437)).
It deletes a batch only once the reply is `ok`, `refused` entries included, because no resend changes those
verdicts; it tells the courier to set the phone's clock automatically when entries come back
`ahead_of_server_clock`. It sends the next batch 2.5 seconds after one is settled, 60 seconds after
`rate_limited`, and after any other refusal or no reply after 5 seconds, doubling up to 5 minutes
([#L21-L30](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L21-L30),
[#L439-L476](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L439-L476)).

## 7. Receive the job

There is no offer to accept or decline. matching assigns a job, automatically to the nearest `online` courier
whose vehicle can carry it, or by an admin's choice, and tells the courier on the channel
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L521-L537),
[#L594-L600](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L594-L600)).
Beside `active_order` from step 2, three pushes concern the courier's job, at any time after the join:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.orderAssigned" />

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.orderUpdated" />

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.orderUnassigned" />

The job's `id` is what every later step sends as `order_id`. The courier app opens the job's screen when a job it
has not shown yet arrives
([tracking_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/tracking_view_model.dart#L133-L155)).
A job's screen changes only for news about its own job: `order_updated` with `cancelled` closes it as cancelled,
`order_unassigned` as taken off the courier, and `active_order` with `null` as no longer active; news about
another job leaves it as it is
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L213-L262),
[order_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/order_view_model.dart#L185-L211)).

## 8. Pick up the parcel

At the merchant, push `pickup` with the job's `id`:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.pickup" />

Because a repeated `pickup` changes nothing, the courier app sends it again until a reply comes, 1, 2, 4 and up
to 15 seconds apart, while the socket is still the courier's
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L39-L44),
[retry_mixin.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/mixins/retry_mixin.dart#L4-L45)),
and shows the proof-of-delivery step once the job's status is `picked_up`
([order_view.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/ui/widgets/order/order_view.dart#L50-L91)).

## 9. Ask for the photo's upload address

Hash the photo's exact bytes with SHA-256 and push `pod_upload` with the job's `id` and the hash:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.podUpload" />

Keep `upload_url` and `upload_headers` for step 10 and `photo_url` for step 11. The courier app checks that the
photo begins as a JPEG does, `FF D8 FF`, before hashing it
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L46-L78)).

## 10. Upload the photo to storage

An HTTP `PUT` of the JPEG's bytes to `upload_url`, unchanged, with the headers of `upload_headers` as they
came, and no token. The storage host checks the signature and the digest, and keeps nothing it refuses. A `412`
means an object already exists under this key; the key is the photo's own SHA-256, so the photo is stored and
the courier goes on to step 11. The courier app counts a `2xx` and a `412` alike as stored
([pod_photo_uploader.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/storage/pod_photo_uploader.dart#L17-L38)).

## 11. Report the delivery

Push `delivered` with the job's `id`, `photo_url` from step 9 as `pod_photo_url`, and the receiver's signature:

<RestSocketMessage id="matching.driverSocket.delivered" />

The courier app takes the signature on the phone's screen and sends it as a PNG
([order_view.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/ui/widgets/order/order_view.dart#L102-L147)).
It sends `delivered` again, as it does `pickup`, while no reply comes and on `proof_unverifiable`
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L135-L181)).
Until the delivery is answered, it keeps `photo_url` and the signature on the phone under the courier and the
job, for at most 24 hours, so a job's screen opened again, after the app was closed, resumes at this step; it
forgets them when the job is cancelled, taken off the courier or no longer active
([proof_draft_store.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/services/storage/proof_draft_store.dart#L17-L112),
[order_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/order_view_model.dart#L173-L211)).

## 12. Go off duty

At the end of the shift, push `status` with `offline`, the message of step 4. matching then offers the courier no
more jobs, because it assigns jobs only to couriers who are `online`
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L207-L223)).
The courier app pushes it when the courier turns duty off while connected
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L113-L125)).
[Signing out](/apis/rest/courier-app/stay-signed-in/) also closes the socket.
