Most of a courier's work goes over matching's driver socket, a Phoenix channel, rather than HTTP. The courier
opens the socket, joins the channel `driver:<driver id>`, goes on duty and sends positions, and receives the
job matching assigns; there is no offer to accept or decline. Carrying the job, the courier reports the pickup,
asks matching for an upload address, puts the proof photo straight into storage over HTTP, and reports the
delivery with the photo and the receiver's signature. Before any of this the courier has
[signed in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/): the driver id is `data.id` from that page's last step, and the
access token must carry the role `courier` and be current when the socket opens
([Stay signed in](/apis/rest/courier-app/stay-signed-in/)).

## 1. Open the driver socket

A WebSocket upgrade at a fixed address. `vsn=2.0.0` selects Phoenix's second frame format, and the access token
goes in `Sec-WebSocket-Protocol` beside the subprotocol `phoenix`, base64-encoded without `=` padding, in place
of `<token>`:

```http
GET /ws/v1/matching/driver?vsn=2.0.0 HTTP/1.1
Host: api.wildanfrananda.my.id
Upgrade: websocket
Connection: Upgrade
Sec-WebSocket-Protocol: phoenix, base64url.bearer.phx.<token>
```

Kong passes this route to matching without checking the token
([gateway.pkl](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-api-gateway/blob/2b38bcc9a795dac8a6a3088b6d67f297fcfa9f5c/config/gateway.pkl#L155-L166)).
matching checks it during the upgrade: an access token identity signed, the role `courier` or `admin`, and an
approved courier record for the token's `sub`
([driver_socket.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_socket.ex#L27-L49)).
It refuses with an empty body: `403` when the token is missing or refused, the role is another, the account has
no courier record or the courier is not approved yet, and `503` when it cannot fetch identity's keys to check
the token. On success it selects the subprotocol `phoenix`
([websocket.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/a27a0320ec8bcc874a8ae93a85f15dfdd5e91e25/lib/phoenix/transports/websocket.ex#L39-L56),
[transport.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/a27a0320ec8bcc874a8ae93a85f15dfdd5e91e25/lib/phoenix/socket/transport.ex#L405-L422)).
The courier app builds this address and these subprotocols
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L75-L86)).
After a `403` it refreshes the session and connects again
([connection_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/connection_repository_impl.dart#L88-L124));
after a `503` or a lost connection it connects again after 1, 2, 4 and up to 30 seconds
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L359-L379)).

## 2. Join the courier's channel

Every frame is a JSON array `[join_ref, ref, topic, event, payload]`; a reply repeats the `ref` of the frame it
answers, and a push from matching has `null` for `ref`
([v2_json_serializer.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/a27a0320ec8bcc874a8ae93a85f15dfdd5e91e25/lib/phoenix/socket/serializers/v2_json_serializer.ex#L63-L98)).
Join the topic `driver:` followed by the driver id, with an empty payload:

```json
["1", "1", "driver:17", "phx_join", {}]
```

matching answers with the courier's duty status, which joining does not change:

```json
["1", "1", "driver:17", "phx_reply", {"status": "ok", "response": {"status": "offline"}}]
```

It refuses the join with `{"status": "error", "response": {"reason": "…"}}`: `invalid_topic` when the id is not
a positive integer, `forbidden` when it is not the courier the socket was opened for, `not_found` or
`pending_approval` when the record is gone or not approved
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L49-L62),
[#L194-L204](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L194-L204)).
Right after the join, matching pushes `active_order` (step 7). The courier app sends every later frame with the
join's `ref` as `join_ref` and a new `ref`
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L136-L161),
[#L244-L271](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L244-L271)).

## 3. Keep the socket alive

matching closes a socket that has received nothing for 60 seconds
([websocket.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/a27a0320ec8bcc874a8ae93a85f15dfdd5e91e25/lib/phoenix/transports/websocket.ex#L26-L35)).
Send a heartbeat on the topic `phoenix` every 30 seconds:

```json
[null, "2", "phoenix", "heartbeat", {}]
```

Phoenix answers it with a `phx_reply` whose status is `ok`
([socket.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix/blob/a27a0320ec8bcc874a8ae93a85f15dfdd5e91e25/lib/phoenix/socket.ex#L705-L714)).
The courier app sends one every 30 seconds from the moment the join succeeds
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L336-L357)).

## 4. Go on duty

Push `status` with `online`:

```json
["1", "3", "driver:17", "status", {"status": "online"}]
```

The reply carries the resulting status: `online`, or `busy` when the courier already holds a job
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L96-L109),
[driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L221-L247)).
A value other than `online`, `busy` and `offline` is refused with `invalid_status`, and `busy` without a job
with `no_active_job`. When the channel ends, because the socket was closed or dropped, matching sets the courier
`offline`
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L258-L261),
[#L290-L293](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L290-L293)),
so a courier on duty sends `status` `online` again after every new join. The courier app does so; the first
time, it asks for location permission and starts the GPS before pushing
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L73-L102),
[#L118-L134](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L118-L134)).

## 5. Send the live position

Push `ping` with the newest position:

```json
["1", "4", "driver:17", "ping", {"latitude": -6.2001, "longitude": 106.8166, "recorded_at": "2026-10-22T03:15:00.123Z", "speed_kmh": 21.6, "bearing_deg": 90.0}]
```

`latitude` and `longitude` are numbers within their ranges. `recorded_at` is an ISO 8601 time with its offset,
such as `Z`, no more than 300 seconds ahead of matching's clock. `speed_kmh`, zero or more, and `bearing_deg`,
from 0 up to but not including 360, may be left out
([telemetry.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/telemetry.ex#L8),
[#L37-L100](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/telemetry.ex#L37-L100),
[driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L85-L93)).
The reply is `ok` with an empty response; a position recorded before the one matching already holds is answered
`ok` and ignored
([#L359-L375](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L359-L375)).
It is refused with `invalid_telemetry` when a field is missing or out of range, and with `rate_limited` beyond
100 pings in 10 seconds from one courier
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L69-L79),
[config.exs](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/config/config.exs#L40-L44)).
Automatic dispatch considers only couriers whose position arrived after they went on duty
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L137-L145),
[dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L529-L537)),
so send a `ping` right after step 4. The courier app sends one at once, then, every 4 seconds, the newest
position when it has moved at least 5 metres
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L21-L22),
[#L199-L209](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L199-L209)).

## 6. Send positions recorded offline

Positions the phone recorded while it had no connection go in `ping_batch`, at most 100 to a batch, each entry
shaped like a `ping` payload:

```json
["1", "5", "driver:17", "ping_batch", {"pings": [
  {"latitude": -6.1932, "longitude": 106.8201, "recorded_at": "2026-10-22T02:58:04.000Z"},
  {"latitude": -6.1937, "longitude": 106.8209, "recorded_at": "2026-10-22T02:58:09.000Z"},
  {"latitude": -6.1702, "longitude": 106.8311, "recorded_at": "2026-10-20T23:10:00.000Z"}
]}]
```

matching keeps them as the courier's history: they never move the courier's live position and do not count for
dispatch
([offline_history.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/offline_history.ex#L1-L6)).
The reply's response sorts the entries by their position in `pings`, counted from 0:

```json
{"stored": [0], "duplicates": [1], "refused": [{"index": 2, "reason": "too_old"}]}
```

`stored` lists the entries kept. `duplicates` lists those whose `recorded_at` matching already holds for the
courier, or that repeat an earlier entry of the batch. `refused` lists those that failed: `invalid_telemetry` as
for `ping`, `ahead_of_server_clock` for more than 300 seconds ahead, and `too_old` for more than 24 hours old
([offline_history.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/offline_history.ex#L31-L84)).
The whole batch is refused with `invalid_batch` when `pings` is missing or not a list, `batch_too_large` above
100 entries, `rate_limited` beyond 30 batches in 60 seconds, and `unavailable` when matching could not store
it, in which case nothing was stored
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L81-L94),
[offline_history.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/offline_history.ex#L101-L123)).
A batch can be sent again: what was stored the first time comes back in `duplicates`. The courier app builds
each entry from the same fields as a `ping`
([channel_client.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L106-L113),
[#L163-L169](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/channel/channel_client.dart#L163-L169)).

## 7. Receive the job

There is no offer to accept or decline. matching assigns a job, automatically to the nearest `online` courier
whose vehicle can carry it, or by an admin's choice, and tells the courier on the channel
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L521-L537),
[#L579-L585](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L579-L585)).
Four pushes concern the courier's job, at any time after the join
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L139-L158)):

- `order_assigned`, with the job: a job was just assigned to this courier.
- `active_order`, with `{"order": <job>}` or `{"order": null}`: the courier's newest job that is `assigned` or
  `picked_up`, sent after every join and after `order_unassigned`
  ([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L225-L232)).
- `order_updated`, with the job: the job moved to `picked_up` or `delivered`, or an admin cancelled it
  ([#L678-L681](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L678-L681)).
- `order_unassigned`, with `{"id": <job id>}`: an admin took the job off this courier
  ([#L375-L386](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L375-L386)).

A job carries its id, status, weight in kilograms, the pickup and drop-off points, and when it was assigned
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L237-L247)):

```json
["1", null, "driver:17", "order_assigned", {"id": 4812, "status": "assigned", "weight_kg": 2, "pickup": {"latitude": -6.1754, "longitude": 106.8272}, "dropoff": {"latitude": -6.2088, "longitude": 106.8456}, "assigned_at": "2026-10-22T02:41:07.512904Z"}]
```

`id` is what every later step sends as `order_id`. The courier app opens the job's screen when a job it has not
shown yet arrives
([tracking_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/viewmodels/tracking_view_model.dart#L131-L150)).

## 8. Pick up the parcel

At the merchant, push `pickup` with the job's `id`:

```json
["1", "6", "driver:17", "pickup", {"order_id": 4812}]
```

The reply is `ok` with an empty response, and matching then pushes `order_updated` with `status` `picked_up`
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L316-L326),
[#L637-L648](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L637-L648)).
It is refused with `invalid_order_id` when `order_id` is missing or not a positive integer, `not_found` when
there is no such job, `forbidden` when the job is another courier's, and `invalid_transition` when the job is
not `assigned`
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L111-L131),
[dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L610-L635)).
A `pickup` for a job already `picked_up` is answered `ok` and changes nothing, so a push whose reply was lost
can be sent again. The courier app shows the proof-of-delivery step once the job's status is `picked_up`
([order_view.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/ui/widgets/order/order_view.dart#L50-L91)).

## 9. Ask for the photo's upload address

Hash the photo's exact bytes with SHA-256 and push `pod_upload` with the job's `id` and the hash in lowercase
hex:

```json
["1", "7", "driver:17", "pod_upload", {"order_id": 4812, "photo_sha256": "bb3b2962c01de34b467fc7ea8ba66d2a142f992f03a6d51c78c690eb883cf18c"}]
```

The job must be `picked_up`. The reply's response holds the upload address, the headers it was signed with, and
the address the photo will have
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L285-L304),
[s3.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/storage/s3.ex#L18-L38)):

```json
{
  "upload_url": "https://storage.wildanfrananda.my.id/example-bucket/pod/4812/bb3b2962c01de34b467fc7ea8ba66d2a142f992f03a6d51c78c690eb883cf18c.jpg?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=…&X-Amz-Date=20261022T025950Z&X-Amz-Expires=600&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host%3Bif-none-match%3Bx-amz-checksum-sha256&X-Amz-Signature=…",
  "upload_headers": {
    "if-none-match": "*",
    "x-amz-checksum-sha256": "uzspYsAd40tGf8fqi6ZtKhQvmS8DptUceMaQ64g88Yw="
  },
  "photo_url": "https://storage.wildanfrananda.my.id/example-bucket/pod/4812/bb3b2962c01de34b467fc7ea8ba66d2a142f992f03a6d51c78c690eb883cf18c.jpg"
}
```

`upload_url` works for 600 seconds. The push is refused with `invalid_photo_hash` when `photo_sha256` is not 64
lowercase hex digits, which is checked first, then with `not_found`, `forbidden`, or `invalid_transition` when
the job is not `picked_up`, and with `invalid_order_id` as for `pickup`
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L116-L122),
[proof.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch/proof.ex#L16-L29)).
The courier app checks that the photo begins as a JPEG does, `FF D8 FF`, before hashing it
([order_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/order_repository_impl.dart#L56-L88)).

## 10. Upload the photo to storage

An HTTP `PUT` of the JPEG's bytes to `upload_url`, unchanged, with the headers of `upload_headers` as they
came, and no token. The storage host checks the signature and the digest, and keeps nothing it refuses. A `412`
means an object already exists under this key; the key is the photo's own SHA-256, so the photo is stored and
the courier goes on to step 11. The courier app counts a `2xx` and a `412` alike as stored
([pod_photo_uploader.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/services/storage/pod_photo_uploader.dart#L17-L38)).

## 11. Report the delivery

Push `delivered` with the job's `id`, `photo_url` from step 9 as `pod_photo_url`, and the receiver's signature
as a PNG in a `data:` URI. Both the photo and the signature are required:

```json
["1", "8", "driver:17", "delivered", {"order_id": 4812, "pod_photo_url": "https://storage.wildanfrananda.my.id/example-bucket/pod/4812/bb3b2962c01de34b467fc7ea8ba66d2a142f992f03a6d51c78c690eb883cf18c.jpg", "pod_signature": "data:image/png;base64,iVBORw0KGgoAAAANSUhEUgAA…"}]
```

Before it accepts, matching reads the stored photo: it must be in this job's folder, carry the checksum its name
promises, and begin as a JPEG does
([dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L450-L486)).
The reply is `ok` with an empty response, and `order_updated` follows with `status` `delivered`; for a job that
belongs to an order, matching then reports the delivery to order by itself, after the reply
([#L390-L403](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L390-L403),
[#L671-L676](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L671-L676)).
A `delivered` for a job already `delivered` is answered `ok` and changes nothing, so a push whose reply was lost
can be sent again. The refusals
([driver_channel.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_channel.ex#L124-L131),
[proof.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch/proof.ex#L51-L64),
[order.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch/order.ex#L109-L129),
[dispatch.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/dispatch.ex#L488-L497)):

- `signature_required`: `pod_signature` is missing or empty.
- `invalid_proof`: the signature is not a PNG in a `data:image/png;base64,` URI; the photo URL is not one of this
  job's; the photo is missing, carries another checksum or is not a JPEG; or the URL is longer than 2,048
  characters or the signature longer than 65,536.
- `proof_unverifiable`: matching could not reach storage to check the photo. Send `delivered` again.
- `invalid_transition`: the job is neither `picked_up` nor `delivered`.
- `unlinked`: the courier record names no account.
- `not_found`, `forbidden` and `invalid_order_id`, as for `pickup`.

The courier app takes the signature on the phone's screen and sends it as a PNG
([order_view.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/ui/widgets/order/order_view.dart#L102-L147)).

## 12. Go off duty

At the end of the shift, push `status` with `offline`:

```json
["1", "9", "driver:17", "status", {"status": "offline"}]
```

matching answers `{"status": "offline"}` and no longer offers the courier jobs, because it assigns jobs only to
couriers who are `online`
([driver_state.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse/tracking/driver_state.ex#L207-L223)).
The courier app pushes it when the courier turns duty off while connected
([duty_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/duty_repository_impl.dart#L104-L116)).
[Signing out](/apis/rest/courier-app/stay-signed-in/) also closes the socket.
