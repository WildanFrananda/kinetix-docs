A courier signs up from the courier app in one call to identity, which creates the account, files the vehicle
with matching and signs the courier in. The account stays a `customer`, and matching does not let the courier
work, until an admin approves the courier in the [backoffice](/apis/rest/backoffice/courier-applications/). The
calls, in order: register with identity, ask matching whether the courier may work, and, once approved, refresh
the session to get the role `courier`. Nothing has to happen before: a new email creates the account, and an
existing customer account is reused with its own password. An approved courier [signs in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/)
from then on.

## 1. Register the account and the vehicle

Every field comes from the courier's input. The courier app checks them against the same rules before sending,
and sends the phone number without spaces, dashes, dots or brackets
([register_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/register_view_model.dart#L44-L49),
[#L112-L158](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/register_view_model.dart#L112-L158)).
It sends `X-Kinetix-Client: courier-app`, so the session is bound to that name and its refreshes must name it
too, and `X-Firebase-AppCheck`, which identity does not read as production is configured;
[signing in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/) says more about both. Keep both tokens and `driverId` from the
`201`: the tokens are a signed-in session for the next steps, and `driverId` is the courier's id in matching, the
topic of the [driver socket](/apis/rest/courier-app/deliver/). The courier app stores the three together and
shows the waiting screen
([session_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/session_repository_impl.dart#L197-L250),
[register_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/register_view_model.dart#L70-L86)).
The answers that carry `accountExists` mean the account exists already; each says what to send again. On a
`429` the courier app counts the wait down as on [signing in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/), and on a `201`
it cannot read it asks the courier to sign in with the same email and password, since the account exists by
then.

## 2. Ask whether the courier may work

Send the access token from step 1. Until an admin approves the courier, matching answers `403` with
`pending_approval`, and its driver socket refuses the courier as well. After approval it answers `200`. With a
stored session, the courier app asks at every start, and again whenever the courier taps "Check again" on the
waiting screen; it stays there while the answer is `pending_approval`, and opens the tracking screen on a `200`
([splash_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/splash_view_model.dart#L20-L48),
[pending_approval_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/viewmodels/pending_approval_view_model.dart#L21-L47)).
When the access token is refused it refreshes once (step 3) and asks again, and it stores the `data.id` of a
`200` as the driver id, in place of the one it held
([session_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/session_repository_impl.dart#L72-L95),
[#L263-L275](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/session_repository_impl.dart#L263-L275)).

## 3. Refresh after approval

Approval makes the account a `courier` in identity
([courier_onboarding_usecase.service.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/4193caf69b2ebea67534e619ebf3a2ccc384e857/src/application/services/courier_onboarding_usecase.service.ts#L76-L87)),
but a token keeps the role it was issued with, so the tokens from step 1 still say `customer`. The driver socket
admits only the roles `courier` and `admin`
([driver_socket.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/7ef67a0ca1d0e8945f638de8a2f9684e84d06886/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_socket.ex#L46-L49))
and refuses the old access token with `403`. A refresh reads the role from the account
([auth_usecase.service.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/4193caf69b2ebea67534e619ebf3a2ccc384e857/src/application/services/auth_usecase.service.ts#L157-L210)):
exchange the refresh token from step 1, with the same `X-Kinetix-Client` as step 1, and use the new pair from then
on. The courier app refreshes when the driver socket refuses its token, then connects again with the new access
token
([connection_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/29a96d33884f3ee9ff2831043e34aee2da235088/lib/repositories/connection_repository_impl.dart#L95-L134)).
