A courier signs up from the courier app in one call to identity, which creates the account, files the vehicle
with matching and signs the courier in. The account stays a `customer`, and matching does not let the courier
work, until an admin approves the courier in the [backoffice](/apis/rest/backoffice/courier-applications/). The
calls, in order: register with identity, ask matching whether the courier may work, and, once approved, refresh
the session to get the role `courier`. Nothing has to happen before: a new email creates the account, and an
existing customer account is reused with its own password. An approved courier [signs in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/)
from then on.

## 1. Register the account and the vehicle

Every field comes from the courier's input. The courier app checks them against the same rules before sending,
and sends the phone number without spaces, dashes, dots or brackets
([register_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/viewmodels/register_view_model.dart#L43-L48),
[#L94-L140](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/viewmodels/register_view_model.dart#L94-L140)).
Keep both tokens and `driverId` from the `201`: the tokens are a signed-in session for the next steps, and
`driverId` is the courier's id in matching, the topic of the [driver socket](/apis/rest/courier-app/deliver/).
The courier app stores the three together
([session_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/session_repository_impl.dart#L143-L177)).
The answers that carry `accountExists` mean the account exists already; each says what to send again.

## 2. Ask whether the courier may work

Send the access token from step 1. Until an admin approves the courier, matching answers `403` with
`pending_approval`, and its driver socket refuses the courier as well. After approval it answers `200`. With a
stored session, the courier app asks at every start and shows a waiting screen while the answer is
`pending_approval`
([splash_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/viewmodels/splash_view_model.dart#L31-L36)).

## 3. Refresh after approval

Approval makes the account a `courier` in identity
([courier_onboarding_usecase.service.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/4b8eb5e5f70f05a8a5f9b6bd0d04b2bf88cc8b7f/src/application/services/courier_onboarding_usecase.service.ts#L63-L74)),
but a token keeps the role it was issued with, so the tokens from step 1 still say `customer`. The driver socket
admits only the roles `courier` and `admin`
([driver_socket.ex](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-matching-service/blob/676e43e0e37879210560fcf7a4dd2f869cae98a5/lib/fleet_pulse_web/channels/driver_socket.ex#L46-L49))
and refuses the old access token with `403`. A refresh reads the role from the account
([auth_usecase.service.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-identity-service/blob/4b8eb5e5f70f05a8a5f9b6bd0d04b2bf88cc8b7f/src/application/services/auth_usecase.service.ts#L108-L128)):
exchange the refresh token from step 1 and use the new pair from then on. The courier app refreshes when the
driver socket refuses its token, then connects again with the new access token
([connection_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/connection_repository_impl.dart#L88-L124)).
