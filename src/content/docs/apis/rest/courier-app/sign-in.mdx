A courier whose account an admin has approved signs in with an email and a password. identity answers a token
pair, or a challenge when the account has two-factor authentication on; the courier app then asks matching for
the courier's record, whose `id` the [driver socket](/apis/rest/courier-app/deliver/) needs. The calls, in
order: sign in, complete the second factor when identity asks for it, read the courier record. The account comes
from [registration](/apis/rest/courier-app/register/).

## 1. Sign in with email and password

The courier app sends the email without surrounding spaces and the password as typed
([login_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/viewmodels/login_view_model.dart#L32-L46),
[session_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/session_repository_impl.dart#L69-L118)).
A `200` carrying `mfaRequired` goes to step 2 with its `challengeToken`; a `200` carrying the token pair goes
to step 3. For an approved courier, `user.role` is `courier`.

<RestEndpoint id="identity.login" />

## 2. Complete two-factor authentication

Only when step 1 answered `mfaRequired`. Send `challengeToken` from that answer and the six digits the courier's
authenticator app shows, within `expiresIn` seconds of step 1. The courier app notes that deadline when the
challenge arrives, and once it has passed asks the courier to sign in again instead of sending the code
([login_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/viewmodels/login_view_model.dart#L65-L79),
[#L110-L118](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/viewmodels/login_view_model.dart#L110-L118)).
The `200` carries the same token pair as a sign-in without a second factor; go on to step 3.

<RestEndpoint id="identity.verifyTwoFactor" />

## 3. Read the courier record

Send the access token from step 1 or step 2. `data.id` is the driver id: the courier's channel on the driver
socket is `driver:<data.id>`. The courier app keeps it with the token pair, then opens the socket
([session_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/86dd282a2b13ef4a4335ac9dba311f313cefea02/lib/repositories/session_repository_impl.dart#L229-L238)).
A `403` with `pending_approval` means an admin has not approved the courier yet, and a `404` means the account
has filed no vehicle; [registration](/apis/rest/courier-app/register/) covers both.
