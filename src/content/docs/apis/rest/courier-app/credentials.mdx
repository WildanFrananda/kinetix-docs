Seal issues a courier a credential of their own record: deliveries, rating and days active on the day it is issued.
With it a courier proves to a verifier, a lender or an employer for example, that the record meets one of the
predicates an admin approved ([Credential predicates](/apis/rest/backoffice/predicates/)), and the verifier learns
nothing else. The proof is made on the phone by a holder runtime, a web page seal serves: the courier app loads it
into a hidden WebView of one pixel
([holder_runtime_view.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/ui/widgets/seal/holder_runtime_view.dart#L10-L16))
and calls it over a JavaScript channel
([holder_runtime.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/services/seal/holder_runtime.dart#L44-L74)).
The credential is bound to a secret the app makes once and keeps in the phone's secure storage
([holder_vault.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/services/seal/holder_vault.dart#L24-L40));
the secret never leaves the phone. Steps 1 to 4 are the app's calls to seal, step 5 has no HTTP at all, and steps 6
and 7 are the verifier's: seal's own verifier page at `/api/v1/seal/verify` makes them
([verifier_routes.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/e0dcc6be63591d63c440b282cf8fc38ecef3f9d5/src/infrastructure/http/verifier_routes.ts#L50-L60)),
and any other verifier can make the same. Steps 3 and 4 follow [signing in](/apis/rest/courier-app/sign-in/) with a
token whose role is `courier`.

## 1. Read the public state

Three reads without a token: the registry's state, the predicates, and where the holder runtime is. The app keeps
all three. Opening the credential screen reads them again when they are more than six hours old, and asking for a
credential reads them again at once
([credential_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/viewmodels/credential_view_model.dart#L28-L47),
[credential_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/repositories/credential_repository_impl.dart#L136-L171)).
The app proves against what it kept, so it asks seal nothing while the courier presents.

## 2. Load the holder runtime

The app opens `entry` in its WebView and waits up to 90 seconds for the page to say it is ready
([holder_runtime.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/services/seal/holder_runtime.dart#L25-L42)),
then asks it to prepare
([credential_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/repositories/credential_repository_impl.dart#L173-L199)):
the page fetches the proving key, the verifier key, the circuit and the parameters for its size from the same build
([holder_runtime.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/e0dcc6be63591d63c440b282cf8fc38ecef3f9d5/holder/src/holder_runtime.ts#L46-L68)).

## 3. Ask for a credential

The app has the runtime compute the holder's public key from the phone's secret, and sends it
([credential_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/repositories/credential_repository_impl.dart#L86-L134)).
Seal counts the courier's numbers from its own copies of matching's deliveries and review's ratings, and records one
credential a day per courier. On a `401` here or in step 4 the app refreshes the session once and asks again
([#L358-L387](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/repositories/credential_repository_impl.dart#L358-L387)).

## 4. Read the credential until it is issued

The app reads its credential again each time the courier opens the credential screen, after step 1, and keeps what
seal answers; when seal cannot be reached it shows the copy it kept
([credential_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/repositories/credential_repository_impl.dart#L45-L84)).
A credential can be presented once it is `issued`.

## 5. Present, without HTTP

The verifier shows a request as a QR code:

```text
kinetix-seal:present?v=1&r=<registry address>&p=<row id>&c=<challenge>&a=<answer address>
```

`r` is the registry's `contractAddress`, `p` a row's `id` from step 1, `c` a fresh challenge of 32 bytes in hex,
and `a` the `https` address the answer goes to
([presentation_request.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/e0dcc6be63591d63c440b282cf8fc38ecef3f9d5/verifier/src/presentation_request.ts#L3-L13)).
The app refuses a request for another registry, a row it does not know, or an answer address that is not `https`
([credential_repository_impl.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/repositories/credential_repository_impl.dart#L201-L299)),
and proves only after the courier has seen the predicate and agreed
([present_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/viewmodels/present_view_model.dart#L47-L90)).
The runtime proves against the snapshot from step 1, binds the proof to the challenge, and answers a transaction
ready for the chain
([#L301-L356](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/repositories/credential_repository_impl.dart#L301-L356)).
The app hands it to the verifier in one of two ways
([presentation_frames.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/services/seal/presentation_frames.dart#L13-L31)):

- **In person**, as QR codes the verifier's camera reads in turn, since the transaction is too large for one. The
  transaction in unpadded base64url is cut into parts of 600 characters, and each frame is
  `KSP1:<tag>:<i>/<n>:<part>`, where `tag` is the first 8 hex characters of the transaction's SHA-256. The app shows
  the frames one after another, a new one every 250 milliseconds
  ([present_view_model.dart](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-courier-mobile/blob/04dd3589634b7a501433810da0fde704d90c5feb/lib/viewmodels/present_view_model.dart#L25)).
- **At a distance**, as a link to the answer address whose fragment is `<challenge>.<transaction>`, the transaction
  again in unpadded base64url. A browser does not send the fragment to a server, so the transaction reaches only the
  page the link opens.

## 6. Hand the presentation to seal

Whoever received the answer sends the transaction to seal, in hex. Seal checks that it is a presentation and nothing
else, and its sponsor wallet pays the fee, so neither the courier nor the verifier needs a wallet. Seal's verifier
page reads step 7 first, in case the challenge is already answered, and sends the transaction only when it is not
([verifier_main.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/e0dcc6be63591d63c440b282cf8fc38ecef3f9d5/verifier/src/verifier_main.ts#L157-L185)).
Kong counts this route on its own, at 20 a minute from one address
([gateway.pkl](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-api-gateway/blob/80ebbdfbfd31be6ab45b40a9a2ce0a377c0ff68f/config/gateway.pkl#L605-L621)).

## 7. Read the verdict

The verifier asks about its challenge until it is answered; seal's page asks every 5 seconds, up to 36 times
([verifier_main.ts](https://github.com/WildanFrananda/kinetix-seal-service/blob/e0dcc6be63591d63c440b282cf8fc38ecef3f9d5/verifier/src/verifier_main.ts#L187-L206)).
The answer names the row the proof met, and the verifier accepts it only when that is the row it asked for.
